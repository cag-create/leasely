## Description (both stores, 4000)

Keycove puts your rentals in your pocket. Landlords, property managers and tenants each get their own home screen, with the things they need and nothing they don't.

FOR LANDLORDS
• See rent status for every unit and get a notification when rent is paid, late, or a payment fails
• Review rental applications as they arrive
• Send leases for e-signature and countersign from your phone
• Track work orders: photos, vendor responses, inspections and completion
• Keep HOA dues on schedule with reminders before they're due
• List a property with photos straight from your camera

FOR PROPERTY MANAGERS
• Work across the properties owners share with you
• Approve vendors, follow repairs and answer applicants on the go
• Your access matches the permissions each owner gives you

FOR TENANTS
• Pay rent and see what's due
• Request a repair and attach photos of the problem in seconds
• Sign your lease and get move-in details
• Get reminders before rent is due

BUILT FOR PHONES
• Push notifications for rent, applications, work orders, leases and HOA dues
• Camera and photo library for work orders and listings
• Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint unlock
• Links from Keycove emails open straight in the app
• Clear offline screen that reconnects by itself

Rent and deposit payments are processed securely by Stripe. Keycove never stores your full card or bank numbers.

A Keycove account is required. Tenants are invited by their landlord or property manager.
