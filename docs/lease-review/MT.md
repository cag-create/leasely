## Early termination rights that override the lease-break fee

### Military service (federal Servicemembers Civil Relief Act)
*50 U.S.C. § 3955*

- **Who:** Tenant may terminate this Lease if Tenant enters military service after signing it; or if Tenant, while in military service, signed this Lease and then receives orders for a permanent change of station (which includes separation or retirement) or to deploy with a military unit or in support of a military operation for 90 days or more; or if Tenant signed this Lease upon such orders and then receives a stop movement order of 30 days or more (or indefinite) that prevents occupancy. A spouse or dependent may terminate within one year after Tenant dies in military service or while performing covered National Guard or Reserve duty, and a Tenant who suffers a catastrophic injury or illness in such service (or the spouse or dependent, if Tenant lacks capacity) may terminate within one year after it occurs. Termination also ends the obligations of Tenant's dependents under this Lease.
- **Notice:** Tenant delivers written notice to Landlord by hand, private business carrier, mail with return receipt requested, or electronic means. Because rent is paid monthly, termination is effective 30 days after the first date on which the next rent payment is due after the notice is delivered; termination after a stop movement order is effective when the notice and orders are delivered.
- **Documentation:** The notice must include a copy of the military orders (official orders, including orders for separation or retirement, or a notification, certification, or verification from the commanding officer).
- **Owes:** Rent is owed only on a prorated basis through the effective date of termination. Landlord may not impose an early termination charge, but Tenant still owes unpaid amounts due under this Lease, including reasonable charges for excess wear. Rent paid in advance for any period after termination will be refunded within 30 days after the effective date. Landlord may not hold Tenant's belongings or security deposit to satisfy a claim for rent accruing after termination.

### Montana National Guard permanent change of duty station (state law)
*Mont. Code Ann. §§ 10-1-905, 10-1-902*

- **Who:** A Tenant who is a member of the Montana Army or Air National Guard serving on active duty (at least 14 consecutive days of full-time state military duty as defined in Mont. Code Ann. § 10-1-1003, or full-time National Guard duty as defined in 32 U.S.C. § 101) may terminate this Lease when ordered to a permanent duty station that is more than 60 miles from Tenant's current residence.
- **Notice:** Tenant delivers written notice of termination to Landlord by hand delivery, U.S. mail, or electronic means. Termination takes effect 45 days after the notice is delivered.
- **Documentation:** The notice must be accompanied by a copy of Tenant's military orders.
- **Owes:** Tenant remains responsible for rent until the termination takes effect, but is not liable for any early termination fee or other damages arising from the early termination.

### Landlord's failure to maintain the Property
*Mont. Code Ann. § 70-24-406(1)(a)*

- **Who:** If Landlord fails to comply with its maintenance duties under Mont. Code Ann. § 70-24-303 in a way that affects health and safety, Tenant may terminate this Lease, unless the condition was caused by Tenant, a member of Tenant's family, or another person on the Property with Tenant's consent.
- **Notice:** Tenant delivers written notice specifying the breach and stating that this Lease will terminate on a date at least 30 days after Landlord receives the notice if the breach is not remedied within 14 days; if Landlord adequately remedies the breach before that date, the Lease does not terminate. In an emergency, Tenant may terminate if Landlord fails to remedy the situation within 3 working days after written notice of the situation and of Tenant's intent to terminate. If substantially the same breach recurs within 6 months after a prior notice, Tenant may terminate on at least 14 days' written notice specifying the breach and the termination date.
- **Owes:** Tenant owes no early termination fee, and Landlord will return all security recoverable under Mont. Code Ann. Title 70, chapter 25. Tenant may also recover actual damages.

### Fire or casualty damage
*Mont. Code Ann. § 70-24-409*

- **Who:** If the Property is damaged or destroyed by fire or casualty to an extent that substantially impairs enjoyment of the dwelling unit, and the damage was not caused by the purposeful or negligent act of Tenant or Tenant's family or guests, Tenant may immediately vacate and terminate this Lease.
- **Notice:** Tenant notifies Landlord in writing within 14 days after vacating of Tenant's intent to terminate; the Lease terminates as of the date Tenant vacated.
- **Owes:** Rent is accounted for as of the date of the fire or casualty. Landlord will return all recoverable security and all prepaid rent.

### Unlawful or harassing entry by Landlord
*Mont. Code Ann. § 70-24-410*

- **Who:** If Landlord makes an unlawful entry, makes a lawful entry in an unreasonable manner, or makes repeated demands for entry that unreasonably harass Tenant, Tenant may terminate this Lease.
- **Notice:** Tenant gives Landlord written notice of termination.
- **Owes:** Tenant owes no early termination fee and may recover actual damages.

### Unlawful ouster or interruption of essential services
*Mont. Code Ann. § 70-24-411*

- **Who:** If Landlord unlawfully removes or excludes Tenant from the Property, or purposefully interrupts heat, running water, hot water, electricity, gas, or other essential services, Tenant may terminate this Lease.
- **Notice:** Tenant gives Landlord written notice of termination.
- **Owes:** Landlord will return all recoverable security and all prepaid rent, and Tenant may recover the greater of three months' periodic rent or treble damages.

### Failure to deliver possession
*Mont. Code Ann. § 70-24-405*

- **Who:** If Landlord fails to deliver possession of the dwelling unit at the start of this Lease, rent abates until possession is delivered and Tenant may terminate this Lease.
- **Notice:** Tenant gives Landlord at least 5 days' written notice of termination.
- **Owes:** Landlord will return all prepaid rent and security.

## Open questions for the reviewing attorney

- SURPRISING — early termination fee cap: § 70-24-201(2)(f) (2025 MCA) limits the aggrieved party's monetary damages for a no-cause early termination to one month's rent or a lower agreed amount, while ALSO entitling the landlord to rent until re-letting or term expiration (with § 70-24-426(3) mitigation). Subsection (2) is prefaced 'Unless the rental agreement provides otherwise,' but (f) itself says the agreed amount 'may not exceed 1 month's rent.' We read this as a hard one-month cap on any early-termination fee/liquidated damages, layered on top of mitigated rent. Attorney should confirm the interplay and that our lease early-termination fee never exceeds one month's rent in Montana.
- Military: § 10-1-905 (enacted 2023) covers only Montana Army/Air National Guard members on 'active duty' as defined in § 10-1-902 (14+ consecutive days of full-time state military duty or full-time Title 32 Guard duty) who receive orders to a permanent duty station more than 60 miles away. Effective 45 days after notice; no early termination fees or other damages. Federal-status servicemembers rely on SCRA (handled by the generator).
- Domestic violence: Montana has NO statutory DV/sexual assault/stalking lease-termination right — confirmed by reading every section of the 2025 MCA chapter 24 (there is no § 70-24-210 despite citations on some websites). § 70-24-111 only addresses notices of no contact. Consider whether Keycove wants a voluntary DV release clause.
- Late fees: no statutory cap or grace period. 'Rent' is defined to include late fees and other agreed charges (§ 70-24-103(14)), so late fees can be part of the 3-day nonpayment notice and deposit deductions (§ 70-25-201(1)). § 70-24-201(4) bars fees based on rent payment type except to recoup an electronic bank fee.
- Deposit: 70-25-202(2) suspends the 30-day/10-day return rule if the lease was terminated under § 70-24-427 and the landlord has a pending court claim. Cleaning deductions require a prior written notice and a 24-hour cure window (§ 70-25-201(3)). Waivers of chapter 25 are void (§ 70-25-103).
- Prohibited provisions: a landlord who knowingly uses a lease with provisions barred by § 70-24-202 (waivers, confession of judgment, exculpation, mandatory email) owes up to 3 months' rent plus actual damages (§ 70-24-403). The builder must not require an email address.
- Fair housing: owner-occupied single-family homes renting no more than three sleeping rooms are exempt from § 49-2-305(1) — relevant to co-living — but the federal FHA advertising ban and local ordinances still apply. Missoula, Bozeman, Helena, Butte-Silver Bow, and Whitefish have local nondiscrimination ordinances that may add sexual orientation and gender identity — not verified here.
- Mold disclosure: the § 70-16-703 statement is optional but gives the landlord liability protection only if provided verbatim, acknowledged by the tenant's signature, and paired with disclosure of known mold and prior testing; disclosure of known mold/testing is itself mandatory under § 70-16-703(2).
- Short notice periods for other breaches (§ 70-24-422): 3 days for unauthorized pets, unauthorized occupants, damage, dangerous activity, or verbal abuse of the landlord; 14 days for other curable breaches; 5 days for a repeat within 6 months.
