Missouri Residential Lease Agreement

Governed by Missouri landlord-tenant law, Mo. Rev. Stat. ch. 441 (Landlord and Tenant) and ch. 535 (Landlord-Tenant Actions)

1. Parties

Landlord: Sample Landlord LLC (100 Main Street, Suite 2)

Tenant: Jordan Sample (jordan@example.com)

2. Property

Landlord leases to Tenant the residential premises at 123 Sample Ave, Apt 4 (the "Property"). Authorized occupants: Tenant only.

3. Term

This Lease begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027. If Tenant stays after the end date with Landlord's consent (including Landlord's acceptance of rent), the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms. Either party may terminate a month-to-month tenancy by written notice stating that the tenancy will end on a periodic rent-paying date not less than one month after the other party receives the notice. (Mo. Rev. Stat. § 441.060)

4. Rent

Tenant shall pay rent of $1,500.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each month.

Payment methods. Tenant shall pay rent by any of these methods accepted by Landlord: Keycove online payment (ACH or card). Rent is paid on the date Landlord receives the funds.

Late fee. If rent is not paid on time, Landlord may charge a late fee of $75.00. The late fee must be a reasonable estimate of Landlord's costs from the late payment and not a penalty.

5. Security Deposit

Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,500.00. The security deposit may not exceed two months' rent. (Mo. Rev. Stat. § 535.300.1)

Landlord shall hold the deposit for Tenant in a bank, credit union, or depository institution insured by an agency of the federal government. Any interest earned on the deposit is Landlord's property. (Mo. Rev. Stat. § 535.300.2)

Within 30 days after the tenancy ends, Landlord shall either return the full deposit or furnish Tenant a written itemized list of the damages for which the deposit or any part is withheld, together with the balance. Landlord complies by mailing the statement and any payment to Tenant's last known address. Landlord may withhold only amounts reasonably necessary to remedy unpaid rent, to restore the unit to its condition at the start of the tenancy (ordinary wear and tear excepted), or to compensate Landlord for actual damages from Tenant's failure to give adequate notice to terminate, provided Landlord makes reasonable efforts to mitigate. If Landlord wrongfully withholds the deposit, Tenant may recover up to twice the amount wrongfully withheld, together with court costs. (Mo. Rev. Stat. § 535.300.3–.5)

Landlord and Tenant will complete a written move-in condition report, with photographs, and a move-out inspection. Items not noted at move-in are presumed to have been in good condition.

6. Utilities

Utilities included in the rent: Water and trash. Tenant is responsible for arranging and paying for all other utilities.

7. Pets and Parking

Pets allowed: No. Assistance animals for a person with a disability are not pets, and no pet fee or pet deposit applies to them. Parking: One assigned space.

8. Landlord Entry

Missouri has no statute setting an entry-notice period. Landlord will give Tenant at least 24 hours' notice before entering for inspections, repairs, or showings, except in an emergency or when Tenant consents at the time of entry.

9. Maintenance and Repairs

Landlord shall maintain the Property in compliance with applicable local building and housing codes and in a condition fit for residential use; Missouri courts imply a warranty of habitability in residential leases. If Landlord fails to maintain the Property, Tenant has statutory remedies, including repair-and-deduct for certain local code violations (§ 441.234) and rent abatement or escrow where a housing-code authority finds the Property unfit (§§ 441.500 to 441.643). (Mo. Rev. Stat. § 441.234; §§ 441.500–441.643; Detling v. Edelbrock, 671 S.W.2d 265 (Mo. banc 1984))

Tenant shall keep the Property clean and safe, use appliances and fixtures only for their intended purposes, not damage or allow damage to the Property beyond normal wear and tear, comply with building and housing codes that apply to tenants, and promptly notify Landlord in writing of any needed repair, water leak, or visible mold. Tenant shall not make alterations, paint, or change or add locks without Landlord's written consent, except as the law allows.

10. Use, Assignment, and Subletting

The Property may be used only as a private residence by Tenant and the authorized occupants. Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Property without Landlord's prior written consent. No illegal activity is permitted on the premises.

11. Default and Eviction

Missouri sets no fixed pay-or-quit period for nonpayment. If rent is due and unpaid after Landlord demands payment, Landlord may bring a rent and possession action under chapter 535 (the one-month notice under § 441.060 is not required before filing) or an unlawful detainer action under chapter 534. (Mo. Rev. Stat. §§ 535.010, 535.020)

Landlord may recover possession only through a court action — a rent and possession action under chapter 535 or an unlawful detainer action under chapter 534. Landlord will not remove or exclude Tenant, remove doors or locks, or interrupt essential services (electric, gas, water, or sewer) without judicial process and a court order; doing so is forcible entry and detainer under § 441.233. (Mo. Rev. Stat. ch. 534; ch. 535; § 441.233)

12. Early Termination by Tenant (Lease Break)

(a) Tenant's early termination option. Tenant may end this Lease before the end date by doing all of the following: (1) giving Landlord written notice of the date Tenant will move out (the "Early Move-Out Date") at least 30 days before that date; (2) paying all rent and other charges due through the Early Move-Out Date; (3) paying an early termination fee of $3,000.00 on or before the Early Move-Out Date; and (4) moving out and returning all keys and access devices by the Early Move-Out Date. If the total rent that would have come due under this Lease after the Early Move-Out Date is less than the early termination fee, Tenant pays that smaller amount instead.

(b) Liquidated damages, not a penalty. Landlord and Tenant agree that if Tenant leaves early, Landlord's damages (rent lost while the Property is vacant and the cost of advertising, showing, screening, and turnover) are difficult to determine at the time of signing, and that the early termination fee is a reasonable estimate of those damages and not a penalty. When Tenant completes every step in paragraph (a), Tenant owes no rent for any period after the Early Move-Out Date, and the fee is Landlord's only remedy for the early termination itself. The fee does not cover unpaid rent through the Early Move-Out Date, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other charges allowed by this Lease and Missouri law.

(c) If Tenant leaves early without using this option. Using paragraph (a) is Tenant's choice. If Tenant moves out before the end date without completing paragraph (a), Landlord will not charge the early termination fee. Instead, Tenant remains responsible for rent as it comes due until the earlier of the end date or the date a replacement tenant's rent begins, plus Landlord's reasonable costs of re-renting. If Tenant leaves early, Landlord shall make reasonable efforts to mitigate damages by re-renting the Property, and rent received from a replacement tenant is credited against what Tenant owes. (Mo. Rev. Stat. § 535.300.4(3))

(d) Rights protected by law: no early termination fee. Paragraphs (a) through (c) do not apply, and Landlord will not charge the early termination fee, when Tenant ends this Lease under any of the following laws. This Lease cannot waive or reduce these rights, and if any part of this Lease conflicts with them, the law controls. Each right has its own notice and documentation requirements:

  • Military service (federal Servicemembers Civil Relief Act) (50 U.S.C. § 3955). Tenant may terminate this Lease if Tenant enters military service after signing it; or if Tenant, while in military service, signed this Lease and then receives orders for a permanent change of station (which includes separation or retirement) or to deploy with a military unit or in support of a military operation for 90 days or more; or if Tenant signed this Lease upon such orders and then receives a stop movement order of 30 days or more (or indefinite) that prevents occupancy. A spouse or dependent may terminate within one year after Tenant dies in military service or while performing covered National Guard or Reserve duty, and a Tenant who suffers a catastrophic injury or illness in such service (or the spouse or dependent, if Tenant lacks capacity) may terminate within one year after it occurs. Termination also ends the obligations of Tenant's dependents under this Lease. Tenant delivers written notice to Landlord by hand, private business carrier, mail with return receipt requested, or electronic means. Because rent is paid monthly, termination is effective 30 days after the first date on which the next rent payment is due after the notice is delivered; termination after a stop movement order is effective when the notice and orders are delivered. The notice must include a copy of the military orders (official orders, including orders for separation or retirement, or a notification, certification, or verification from the commanding officer). Rent is owed only on a prorated basis through the effective date of termination. Landlord may not impose an early termination charge, but Tenant still owes unpaid amounts due under this Lease, including reasonable charges for excess wear. Rent paid in advance for any period after termination will be refunded within 30 days after the effective date. Landlord may not hold Tenant's belongings or security deposit to satisfy a claim for rent accruing after termination.
  • Military service (Missouri law) (Mo. Rev. Stat. § 41.944). A member of the U.S. Armed Forces on active duty, or a member of the Missouri Army or Air National Guard serving on full-time duty or as a civil service technician with a Missouri National Guard unit, who is a tenant of this residential dwelling may terminate this Lease if the member: (1) receives permanent change of station orders; (2) receives temporary duty orders in excess of 90 days assigning the member to a location at least 25 miles from the Property; (3) is discharged or released from active duty or from full-time or technician status; or (4) is ordered to reside in government-supplied quarters. Tenant serves Landlord written notice of termination effective on a date stated in the notice that is not less than 15 days after the notice is served. Before the termination date, Tenant furnishes Landlord a copy of the official orders or a signed letter from Tenant's commanding officer confirming the orders. The final lease payment is due by the effective termination date and is payable when it would otherwise have been due under this Lease. Landlord will not charge any early termination fee, and Tenant is entitled to the full return of any security deposit and pet deposit if Tenant has otherwise complied with this Lease and § 535.300.
  • Victims of domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking (Mo. Rev. Stat. § 441.920). A Tenant who is, has been, or is in imminent danger of becoming a victim of domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking (as defined in § 455.010) may not be denied tenancy, evicted, or found in violation of this Lease on that basis, and has an affirmative defense to liability for rent for the period after Tenant vacates the Property if a court finds Tenant was such a victim and Tenant notified Landlord and provided the required documentation. This protection does not apply if Tenant allowed the named perpetrator into the premises, or if Landlord reasonably believes the perpetrator poses a threat to the safety of other occupants or the property. This right applies only to residential property. Tenant provides Landlord a written statement that Tenant is, was, or is in imminent danger of becoming a victim of domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking, and vacates the Property. If Landlord requests, Tenant provides documentation: either a document signed under penalty of perjury by an employee of a victim service provider or a health or mental health professional from whom Tenant sought assistance (and signed by Tenant), or a record of a law enforcement agency, court, or administrative agency about the incident. Submitting false information is a basis for denial of tenancy, eviction, or a lease violation. Tenant is liable for rent through the date Tenant vacates and owes no rent for the period after vacating. Missouri law permits Landlord to impose a reasonable termination fee, stated in this Lease, on a Tenant who terminates under this right (§ 441.920.6). These protections cannot be waived.

(e) Security deposit. After Tenant moves out, Landlord may apply the security deposit to an unpaid early termination fee or to rent owed under paragraph (c) to the extent the law allows, and will account for the deposit as described in the Security Deposit section and as the law requires.
