Virginia Co-Living / Room Rental Agreement

Room rental in a shared residence. Virginia landlord-tenant law, including its eviction procedure, applies in full.

1. Parties

Landlord: Sample Landlord LLC (100 Main Street, Suite 2)

Tenant: Jordan Sample (jordan@example.com)

2. Nature of This Agreement

Tenant rents Bedroom B (the "Room") at 123 Sample Ave, Apt 4 (the "Residence") and may use the shared common areas. Authorized occupants of the Room: Tenant only.

Landlord may move Tenant to a comparable room in the Residence only with Tenant's written consent or on at least 30 days' written notice with no increase in rent. The "co-living" and "room rental" terms of this Agreement govern house rules and room assignment only. Tenant has the rights of a residential tenant under Virginia law. If Landlord seeks to remove Tenant from possession, Landlord will use the court eviction process described in this Agreement and will never use self-help removal.

3. Term

This Agreement begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027. If Tenant stays after the end date with Landlord's consent (including Landlord's acceptance of rent), the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms. Either Landlord or Tenant may end a month-to-month tenancy by serving written notice on the other at least 30 days before the next rent due date (seven days before the next rent due date for a week-to-week tenancy). If Tenant stays on month-to-month with Landlord's agreement, the terms of this Lease continue, except that Landlord may change the rent by written notice that takes effect on the next rent due date at least 30 days after the notice. (Va. Code Ann. § 55.1-1253(A), (D))

4. Rent

Tenant shall pay rent of $1,500.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each month. Rent covers the Room, use of the common areas, and these shared utilities: Water and trash.

Payment methods. Tenant shall pay rent by any of these methods accepted by Landlord: Keycove online payment (ACH or card). Rent is paid on the date Landlord receives the funds.

Late fee. If rent is not paid on time, Landlord may charge a late fee of $75.00. A late charge may be charged only because this written Lease provides for it, and it may not exceed the lesser of 10% of the monthly rent or 10% of the remaining balance due and owed by Tenant. (Va. Code Ann. § 55.1-1204(E))

Within 45 days after the later of the end of the tenancy or the date Tenant vacates, Landlord shall give Tenant a written notice itemizing the deposit and any deductions, together with any amount due to Tenant. Landlord may apply the deposit only to accrued rent (including reasonable late charges stated in this Lease), damage caused by Tenant's failure to meet Tenant's duties under Va. Code § 55.1-1227 less reasonable wear and tear, other charges provided in this Lease, and actual damages for breach under § 55.1-1251. Within five days after Landlord receives notice that Tenant intends to vacate (or when Landlord asks Tenant to vacate), Landlord will notify Tenant in writing of Tenant's right to be present at the move-out inspection; if Tenant asks in writing to attend, the inspection will be held within 72 hours after Tenant delivers possession. Within five days after Tenant moves in, Landlord shall give Tenant a written report itemizing any damage to the Property existing at move-in, including whether there is any visible evidence of mold in readily accessible interior areas. The report is deemed correct unless Tenant objects to it in writing within five days after receiving it.

11. Default and Eviction

If rent is not paid when due, Landlord may serve Tenant with a written notice stating that the rent is unpaid and that Landlord will terminate this Lease if the rent is not paid within 14 days. If Tenant does not pay within 14 days after the notice is served, Landlord may terminate this Lease and seek possession through the court. The same 14-day notice applies if a rent check is returned for insufficient funds or an electronic payment is rejected, in which case payment must be made by cash, cashier's check, certified check, or a completed electronic funds transfer. (Va. Code Ann. § 55.1-1245(F))

Landlord may recover possession only through an unlawful detainer action in general district court and a writ of eviction. Landlord will not remove or exclude Tenant, interrupt an essential service, refuse Tenant access, or take action to make the Property unsafe in order to regain possession. (Va. Code Ann. §§ 8.01-126, 55.1-1252, 55.1-1243.1)
