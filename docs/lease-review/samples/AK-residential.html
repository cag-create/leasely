Alaska Residential Lease Agreement

Governed by Alaska Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act, AS 34.03.010 - 34.03.380

1. Parties

Landlord: Sample Landlord LLC (100 Main Street, Suite 2)

Tenant: Jordan Sample (jordan@example.com)

2. Property

Landlord leases to Tenant the residential premises at 123 Sample Ave, Apt 4 (the "Property"). Authorized occupants: Tenant only.

3. Term

This Lease begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027. If Tenant stays after the end date with Landlord's consent (including Landlord's acceptance of rent), the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms. While rent is current, Landlord or Tenant may end a month-to-month tenancy by written notice given to the other at least 30 days before the rental due date specified in the notice. A week-to-week tenancy may be ended by written notice at least 14 days before the termination date. (AS 34.03.290(a), (b))

4. Rent

Tenant shall pay rent of $1,500.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each month.

Payment methods. Tenant shall pay rent by any of these methods accepted by Landlord: Keycove online payment (ACH or card). Rent is paid on the date Landlord receives the funds.

Late fee. If rent is not paid on time, Landlord may charge a late fee of $75.00. The late fee must be a reasonable estimate of Landlord's costs from the late payment and not a penalty.

5. Security Deposit

Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,500.00. Landlord may not demand or receive prepaid rent or a security deposit, however denominated, totaling more than two months' periodic rent. This cap does not apply if the rent exceeds $2,000 a month. If Tenant keeps a pet on the Property that is not a service animal, Landlord may also require an additional pet deposit of no more than one month's rent, which must be accounted for separately and may be applied only to damages directly related to the pet. 'Prepaid rent' does not include the first month's rent. (AS 34.03.070(a), (h); AS 34.03.360(16))

Landlord will promptly deposit all prepaid rent and security deposits, wherever practicable, in a trust account in a bank, savings and loan association, or licensed escrow agent. Landlord may keep prepaid rent and security deposits in a single account but will separately account for each tenant's prepaid rent and security deposit, will not commingle them with other funds, and will not use one tenant's money for another tenant's refund, rent, or damages. The terms and conditions under which the prepaid rent or deposit may be withheld are stated in this Lease. (AS 34.03.070(c))

When the tenancy ends, Landlord may apply the deposit and prepaid rent only to accrued rent and to damages Landlord has suffered from Tenant's failure to meet Tenant's obligations under AS 34.03.120, not including normal wear and tear or deterioration caused by Landlord's failure to prepare for expected conditions or to meet Landlord's own obligations. Landlord will itemize the accrued rent and damages in a written notice mailed to Tenant's last known address, together with any amount due to Tenant. If Landlord or Tenant gave proper termination notice under AS 34.03.290, Landlord will mail the notice and refund within 14 days after the tenancy ends and Tenant delivers possession, or within 30 days if Landlord deducts for damage caused by Tenant's noncompliance. If Tenant did not give proper notice, Landlord will mail them within 30 days after the tenancy ends, Tenant delivers possession, or Landlord learns the unit is abandoned. If Landlord does not know Tenant's mailing address but knows how to contact Tenant, Landlord will make a reasonable effort to deliver the notice and refund. If Landlord wilfully fails to comply, Tenant may recover up to twice the amount wrongfully withheld. (AS 34.03.070(b), (d), (g))

If Landlord requires one, Landlord and Tenant will complete a premises condition statement describing the condition of the premises, including fixtures, at the start of the tenancy, and, if the Property is furnished, a contents inventory describing each item and its condition. When signed by both parties, they become part of this Lease, Tenant will acknowledge their accuracy by signature, and they are presumptive evidence of the condition of the Property and its contents at the start of the tenancy.

Tenant will not unreasonably withhold consent for Landlord to enter to inspect the Property, make necessary or agreed repairs, decorations, alterations, or improvements, supply necessary or agreed services, remove Landlord's personal property not covered by this Lease, or show the unit to prospective or actual purchasers, mortgagees, tenants, workers, or contractors. (AS 34.03.140; AS 34.03.230(b))

Landlord will make all repairs and do whatever is necessary to put and keep the Property in a fit and habitable condition; keep common areas clean and safe; maintain in good and safe working order all electrical, plumbing, sanitary, heating, ventilating, air-conditioning, kitchen, and other facilities and appliances Landlord supplies or is required to supply; provide and arrange removal of garbage and waste receptacles; supply running water and reasonable amounts of hot water and heat at all times, insofar as energy conditions permit (except where the tenant controls a direct utility installation or the parties validly waive water service for a property without a well or utility connection); provide locks and keys on Tenant's request; and provide smoke and carbon monoxide detection devices as required by AS 18.70.095. (AS 34.03.100)

Tenant shall keep the Property clean and safe, use appliances and fixtures only for their intended purposes, not damage or allow damage to the Property beyond normal wear and tear, comply with building and housing codes that apply to tenants, and promptly notify Landlord in writing of any needed repair, water leak, or visible mold. Tenant shall not make alterations, paint, or change or add locks without Landlord's written consent, except as the law allows.

The Property may be used only as a private residence by Tenant and the authorized occupants. Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Property without Landlord's prior written consent. No illegal activity is permitted on the premises.

11. Default and Eviction

If rent is unpaid when due and Tenant does not pay it in full within seven days after Landlord's written notice of nonpayment and of Landlord's intention to terminate this Lease if rent is not paid within that time, the tenancy terminates unless Landlord agrees to let Tenant remain. If Landlord serves the notice by mail, three days are added to that period. Only one written notice is needed for any one default, and Landlord may accept a partial payment and extend the eviction date accordingly. (AS 34.03.220(b); AS 09.45.090(a)(1), (c); AS 09.45.100)

Landlord may recover possession of the Property only through a forcible entry and detainer action in court under AS 09.45.060 - 09.45.160, after serving the written notice to quit the law requires, or in case of abandonment or surrender. Landlord will not take possession by any other means, including by wilfully interrupting electricity, gas, water, sanitary, or other essential services. (AS 34.03.280; AS 09.45.060 - 09.45.160)

12. Early Termination by Tenant (Lease Break)

(a) Tenant's early termination option. Tenant may end this Lease before the end date by doing all of the following: (1) giving Landlord written notice of the date Tenant will move out (the "Early Move-Out Date") at least 30 days before that date; (2) paying all rent and other charges due through the Early Move-Out Date; (3) paying an early termination fee of $3,000.00 on or before the Early Move-Out Date; and (4) moving out and returning all keys and access devices by the Early Move-Out Date. If the total rent that would have come due under this Lease after the Early Move-Out Date is less than the early termination fee, Tenant pays that smaller amount instead.

(b) Liquidated damages, not a penalty. Landlord and Tenant agree that if Tenant leaves early, Landlord's damages (rent lost while the Property is vacant and the cost of advertising, showing, screening, and turnover) are difficult to determine at the time of signing, and that the early termination fee is a reasonable estimate of those damages and not a penalty. When Tenant completes every step in paragraph (a), Tenant owes no rent for any period after the Early Move-Out Date, and the fee is Landlord's only remedy for the early termination itself. The fee does not cover unpaid rent through the Early Move-Out Date, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other charges allowed by this Lease and Alaska law.

(c) If Tenant leaves early without using this option. Using paragraph (a) is Tenant's choice. If Tenant moves out before the end date without completing paragraph (a), Landlord will not charge the early termination fee. Instead, Tenant remains responsible for rent as it comes due until the earlier of the end date or the date a replacement tenant's rent begins, plus Landlord's reasonable costs of re-renting. If Tenant abandons the dwelling unit, Landlord will make reasonable efforts to rent it at a fair rental value. If Landlord re-rents it for a term beginning before this Lease would have ended, this Lease ends on the date the new tenancy begins. If Landlord fails to use reasonable efforts to re-rent, or accepts the abandonment as a surrender, this Lease is considered terminated by Landlord on the date Landlord has notice of the abandonment. Each party has a duty to mitigate damages. (AS 34.03.230(c); AS 34.03.320)

(d) Rights protected by law: no early termination fee. Paragraphs (a) through (c) do not apply, and Landlord will not charge the early termination fee, when Tenant ends this Lease under any of the following laws. This Lease cannot waive or reduce these rights, and if any part of this Lease conflicts with them, the law controls. Each right has its own notice and documentation requirements:

Tenant may terminate this Lease if Tenant enters military service after signing it; or if Tenant, while in military service, signed this Lease and then receives orders for a permanent change of station (which includes separation or retirement) or to deploy with a military unit or in support of a military operation for 90 days or more; or if Tenant signed this Lease upon such orders and then receives a stop movement order of 30 days or more (or indefinite) that prevents occupancy. A spouse or dependent may terminate within one year after Tenant dies in military service or while performing covered National Guard or Reserve duty, and a Tenant who suffers a catastrophic injury or illness in such service (or the spouse or dependent, if Tenant lacks capacity) may terminate within one year after it occurs. Termination also ends the obligations of Tenant's dependents under this Lease. Tenant delivers written notice to Landlord by hand, private business carrier, mail with return receipt requested, or electronic means. Because rent is paid monthly, termination is effective 30 days after the first date on which the next rent payment is due after the notice is delivered; termination after a stop movement order is effective when the notice and orders are delivered. The notice must include a copy of the military orders (official orders, including orders for separation or retirement, or a notification, certification, or verification from the commanding officer). Rent is owed only on a prorated basis through the effective date of termination. Landlord may not impose an early termination charge, but Tenant still owes unpaid amounts due under this Lease, including reasonable charges for excess wear. Rent paid in advance for any period after termination will be refunded within 30 days after the effective date. Landlord may not hold Tenant's belongings or security deposit to satisfy a claim for rent accruing after termination.
  • Alaska organized militia on state active duty (AS 26.05.135(a); AS 26.05.010(b); 50 U.S.C. § 3955). Alaska law applies the consumer-transaction and lease protections of the federal Servicemembers Civil Relief Act to members of the organized militia (the Alaska National Guard, the Alaska Naval Militia, and the Alaska State Defense Force) while on active duty for the state by order of the governor. Such a Tenant may terminate this Lease on the same terms the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act provides for federal military service, including after entering that duty during the term of this Lease or, if Tenant signed this Lease while on that duty, after receiving orders for a permanent change of station or a deployment of at least 90 days. Tenant delivers written notice of termination to Landlord or Landlord's agent by hand delivery, private business carrier, return-receipt mail, or an agreed electronic method. For a lease with monthly rent, termination takes effect 30 days after the first date on which the next rental payment is due after the notice is delivered. The notice must include a copy of the governor's order or other military orders placing Tenant on state active duty. Unpaid rent for the period before the termination date is prorated. Landlord may not impose an early termination charge, but Tenant remains responsible for other obligations due under this Lease at termination, including reasonable charges for excess wear.   • Landlord's material noncompliance (AS 34.03.160(a), (d)). If Landlord materially fails to comply with this Lease, or fails to comply with AS 34.03.100 in a way that materially affects health and safety, Tenant may terminate this Lease. If Landlord fails to deliver possession of the dwelling unit at the start of the term, rent abates until possession is delivered and Tenant may terminate this Lease. (e) Security deposit. After Tenant moves out, Landlord may apply the security deposit to an unpaid early termination fee or to rent owed under paragraph (c) to the extent the law allows, and will account for the deposit as described in the Security Deposit section and as the law requires.
