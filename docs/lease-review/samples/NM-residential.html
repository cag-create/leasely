2. Property

Landlord leases to Tenant the residential premises at 123 Sample Ave, Apt 4 (the "Property"). Authorized occupants: Tenant only.

3. Term

This Lease begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027. If Tenant stays after the end date with Landlord's consent (including Landlord's acceptance of rent), the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms. Either Landlord or Tenant may terminate a month-to-month residency by written notice given to the other at least 30 days before the periodic rental date specified in the notice. A week-to-week residency requires at least 7 days' written notice. Landlord may increase rent in a month-to-month residency only by written notice at least 30 days before the periodic rental date, and may increase a fee provided for in this Lease only by written notice at least 60 days before the periodic rental date (or, for a fixed term, before the end of the term). (NMSA 1978, §§ 47-8-37(A)–(B), 47-8-15(F), 47-8-19.4)

Tenant shall pay rent of $1,500.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each month.

Payment methods. Tenant shall pay rent by any of these methods accepted by Landlord: Keycove online payment (ACH or card). Rent is paid on the date Landlord receives the funds.

Late fee. If rent is not paid on time, Landlord may charge a late fee of $75.00. may be charged only if this Lease provides for it, may not exceed 5% of the rent for each rental period in which Tenant is in default, and is calculated only on rent, not on deposits, other fees, or utilities. To assess a late fee, Landlord must notify Tenant of the late fee charged no later than the last day of the rental period immediately following the period in which the default occurred. (NMSA 1978, § 47-8-15(D))

Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,500.00. For a rental agreement with a term of less than one year, the deposit may not exceed one month's rent. Landlord and Tenant will complete a written move-in condition report, with photographs, and a move-out inspection. Items not noted at move-in are presumed to have been in good condition.

If the total rent that would have come due under this Lease after the Early Move-Out Date is less than the early termination fee, Tenant pays that smaller amount instead.

(b) Liquidated damages, not a penalty. Landlord and Tenant agree that if Tenant leaves early, Landlord's damages (rent lost while the Property is vacant and the cost of advertising, showing, screening, and turnover) are difficult to determine at the time of signing, and that the early termination fee is a reasonable estimate of those damages and not a penalty. When Tenant completes every step in paragraph (a), Tenant owes no rent for any period after the Early Move-Out Date, and the fee is Landlord's only remedy for the early termination itself. The fee does not cover unpaid rent through the Early Move-Out Date, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other charges allowed by this Lease and New Mexico law.

(c) If Tenant leaves early without using this option. Using paragraph (a) is Tenant's choice. If Tenant moves out before the end date without completing paragraph (a), Landlord will not charge the early termination fee. Instead, Tenant remains responsible for rent as it comes due until the earlier of the end date or the date a replacement tenant's rent begins, plus Landlord's reasonable costs of re-renting. If Tenant leaves before the end of the term, Landlord has a duty to mitigate its damages, including by making reasonable efforts to re-rent the Property at a fair rental.
