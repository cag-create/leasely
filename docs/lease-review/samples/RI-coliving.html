Room rental in a shared residence. Rhode Island landlord-tenant law, including its eviction procedure, applies in full.

Landlord: Sample Landlord LLC (100 Main Street, Suite 2)

2. Nature of This Agreement

Tenant rents Bedroom B (the "Room") at 123 Sample Ave, Apt 4 (the "Residence") and may use the shared common areas. Authorized occupants of the Room: Tenant only.

Landlord may move Tenant to a comparable room in the Residence only with Tenant's written consent or on at least 30 days' written notice with no increase in rent. The "co-living" and "room rental" terms of this Agreement govern house rules and room assignment only. Tenant has the rights of a residential tenant under Rhode Island law. If Landlord seeks to remove Tenant from possession, Landlord will use the court eviction process described in this Agreement and will never use self-help removal.

3. Term

This Agreement begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027. If Tenant stays after the end date with Landlord's consent (including Landlord's acceptance of rent), the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms. Landlord or Tenant may end a month-to-month tenancy by written notice delivered to the other at least 30 days before the termination date stated in the notice (10 days for a week-to-week tenancy; three months before the end of the occupation year for a year-to-year tenancy). Landlord must give at least 60 days' written notice before a rent increase takes effect, and at least 120 days' notice before raising the rent of a month-to-month tenant who is over 62. (R.I. Gen. Laws §§ 34-18-37, 34-18-16.1)

4. Rent

Payment methods. Tenant shall pay rent by any of these methods accepted by Landlord: Keycove online payment (ACH or card). Rent is paid on the date Landlord receives the funds.

Late fee. If rent is not paid on time, Landlord may charge a late fee of $75.00. The late fee must be a reasonable estimate of Landlord's costs from the late payment and not a penalty.

Within 20 days after the latest of the end of the tenancy, Tenant's delivery of possession, or Tenant's providing a forwarding address for the deposit, Landlord shall deliver to Tenant the deposit minus only unpaid accrued rent, reasonable cleaning and trash disposal expenses, and physical damage beyond ordinary wear and tear caused by Tenant's noncompliance with R.I. If Landlord does not comply, Tenant may recover the amount due plus damages equal to twice the amount wrongfully withheld and reasonable attorney's fees. (R.I. Gen. Laws § 34-18-19(b)–(c), (f))

Landlord and Tenant will complete a written move-in condition report, with photographs, and a move-out inspection. Items not noted at move-in are presumed to have been in good condition.

11. Default and Eviction

If any part of the rent is 15 days in arrears, Landlord shall send Tenant a written demand, in the statutory form, stating the amount 15 days in arrears and that the rental agreement will terminate unless Tenant pays within five days of the date the notice is mailed. Landlord may file a complaint for eviction for nonpayment no earlier than the sixth day after mailing. Tenant may cure by paying the full rent due before suit is filed and, if Tenant has not received such a notice in the preceding six months, by paying all rent in arrears plus court costs at the hearing. (R.I. Gen. Laws § 34-18-35)

Landlord may recover possession only through an eviction action filed in the appropriate District Court or housing court using the statutory complaint and summons forms, after any required demand or termination notice, and execution of a court judgment. Landlord will not recover possession by any other means, including by willfully interrupting heat, running water, hot water, electricity, gas, or other essential services, except after abandonment or surrender. (a) Tenant's early termination option. Tenant may end this Agreement before the end date by doing all of the following: (1) giving Landlord written notice of the date Tenant will move out (the "Early Move-Out Date") at least 30 days before that date; (2) paying all rent and other charges due through the Early Move-Out Date; (3) paying an early termination fee of $3,000.00 on or before the Early Move-Out Date; and (4) moving out and returning all keys and access devices by the Early Move-Out Date. If the total rent that would have come due under this Agreement after the Early Move-Out Date is less than the early termination fee, Tenant pays that smaller amount instead.

(b) Liquidated damages, not a penalty. Landlord and Tenant agree that if Tenant leaves early, Landlord's damages (rent lost while the Room is vacant and the cost of advertising, showing, screening, and turnover) are difficult to determine at the time of signing, and that the early termination fee is a reasonable estimate of those damages and not a penalty. When Tenant completes every step in paragraph (a), Tenant owes no rent for any period after the Early Move-Out Date, and the fee is Landlord's only remedy for the early termination itself. The fee does not cover unpaid rent through the Early Move-Out Date, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other charges allowed by this Agreement and Rhode Island law.

(c) If Tenant leaves early without using this option. Using paragraph (a) is Tenant's choice. If Tenant moves out before the end date without completing paragraph (a), Landlord will not charge the early termination fee. Instead, Tenant remains responsible for rent as it comes due until the earlier of the end date or the date a replacement tenant's rent begins, plus Landlord's reasonable costs of re-renting. If Tenant abandons the Property, Landlord will send a certified letter to Tenant's last known address stating that Landlord will re-rent unless Tenant replies within seven days, and will then make reasonable efforts to re-rent the Property at a fair rental. If Landlord re-rents for a term beginning before this Lease ends, this Lease ends when the new tenancy begins, and rent received from the replacement tenant is credited against what Tenant owes.
