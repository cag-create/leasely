Landlord: Sample Landlord LLC (100 Main Street, Suite 2)

2. Property

Landlord leases to Tenant the residential premises at 123 Sample Ave, Apt 4 (the "Property"). Authorized occupants: Tenant only.

3. Term

This Lease begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027. If Tenant stays after the end date with Landlord's consent (including Landlord's acceptance of rent), the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms. A month-to-month tenancy may be ended by at least 30 days' written notice (7 days' written notice for a week-to-week tenancy). (735 ILCS 5/9-207)

4. Rent

Tenant shall pay rent of $1,500.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each month.

Payment methods. Tenant shall pay rent by any of these methods accepted by Landlord: Keycove online payment (ACH or card). Rent is paid on the date Landlord receives the funds.

Late fee. If rent is not paid on time, Landlord may charge a late fee of $75.00. The late fee must be a reasonable estimate of Landlord's costs from the late payment and not a penalty.

5. Security Deposit

Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,500.00. Illinois state law sets no maximum amount for a security deposit.

If the property has 25 or more units in one building or a complex of buildings on contiguous parcels, Landlord shall pay Tenant interest on any deposit held more than 6 months, at the rate the largest commercial bank headquartered in Illinois paid on minimum-deposit passbook savings accounts on December 31 of the year before the Lease began, paid within 30 days after the end of each 12-month rental period (in cash or as a credit against rent) once it reaches $5, and in full at the end of the tenancy. If Landlord transfers the property, the new owner becomes liable for the deposit and any interest. (765 ILCS 715/1–715/3; 765 ILCS 710/1.1)

Landlord may withhold any part of the deposit for property damage only if, within 30 days after Tenant vacates or Tenant's right of possession ends (whichever is later), Landlord delivers to Tenant in person, by postmarked mail to Tenant's last known address, or by email to a verified email address Tenant provided, an itemized statement of the damage and the estimated or actual cost to repair or replace each item, with paid receipts or copies attached. If estimated costs are given, Landlord shall furnish the paid receipts within 30 days after the statement. If Landlord does not furnish the statement and receipts as required, Landlord shall return the full deposit within 45 days after Tenant vacates. Landlord may use the reasonable cost of Landlord's own labor. A charge for a specific component may be withheld at the dollar amount stated in this Lease only if it is for damage beyond normal wear and tear and the statement references that amount and includes a copy of the applicable Lease provision. (765 ILCS 710/1)

Landlord and Tenant will complete a written move-in condition report, with photographs, and a move-out inspection. Items not noted at move-in are presumed to have been in good condition.

6. Utilities

Utilities included in the rent: Water and trash. Tenant is responsible for arranging and paying for all other utilities.

9. Maintenance and Repairs

Landlord will keep the Premises fit for habitation and in compliance with applicable building and housing codes throughout the Lease. If a repair required by this Lease or by law costs no more than the lesser of $500 or one-half of the monthly rent and Landlord does not make it within 14 days after Tenant's written notice by certified or registered mail (or sooner in an emergency), Tenant may have it made by a licensed, insured tradesperson and deduct the paid bill from rent, unless Tenant caused the condition. (765 ILCS 742/5 (Residential Tenants' Right to Repair Act); Illinois implied warranty of habitability (case law))

Tenant shall keep the Property clean and safe, use appliances and fixtures only for their intended purposes, not damage or allow damage to the Property beyond normal wear and tear, comply with building and housing codes that apply to tenants, and promptly notify Landlord in writing of any needed repair, water leak, or visible mold. Tenant shall not make alterations, paint, or change or add locks without Landlord's written consent, except as the law allows.

10. Use, Assignment, and Subletting

The Property may be used only as a private residence by Tenant and the authorized occupants. Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Property without Landlord's prior written consent. No illegal activity is permitted on the premises.

11. Default and Eviction

Any time after rent is due, Landlord may demand payment in writing and notify Tenant that the Lease will end unless payment is made within a stated time of not less than 5 days after service. To avoid invalidation by partial payment, the notice must state: "Only FULL PAYMENT of the rent demanded in this notice will waive the landlord's right to terminate the lease under this notice, unless the landlord agrees in writing to continue the lease in exchange for receiving partial payment." For other lease defaults, Landlord may give a 10-day notice to quit. (735 ILCS 5/9-209, 9-210, 9-211)

Landlord may recover possession only by filing an eviction action in the circuit court under the Illinois Eviction Act and obtaining a court order enforced by the sheriff. Landlord will not use self-help, such as changing locks, removing Tenant's belongings, or shutting off utilities, to remove Tenant. (735 ILCS 5/9-102, 9-106)

12. Early Termination by Tenant (Lease Break)

(a) Tenant's early termination option. Tenant may end this Lease before the end date by doing all of the following: (1) giving Landlord written notice of the date Tenant will move out (the "Early Move-Out Date") at least 30 days before that date; (2) paying all rent and other charges due through the Early Move-Out Date; (3) paying an early termination fee of $3,000.00 on or before the Early Move-Out Date; and (4) moving out and returning all keys and access devices by the Early Move-Out Date. If the total rent that would have come due under this Lease after the Early Move-Out Date is less than the early termination fee, Tenant pays that smaller amount instead.

(b) Liquidated damages, not a penalty. Landlord and Tenant agree that if Tenant leaves early, Landlord's damages (rent lost while the Property is vacant and the cost of advertising, showing, screening, and turnover) are difficult to determine at the time of signing, and that the early termination fee is a reasonable estimate of those damages and not a penalty. When Tenant completes every step in paragraph (a), Tenant owes no rent for any period after the Early Move-Out Date, and the fee is Landlord's only remedy for the early termination itself. The fee does not cover unpaid rent through the Early Move-Out Date, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other charges allowed by this Lease and Illinois law.

(c) If Tenant leaves early without using this option. Using paragraph (a) is Tenant's choice. If Tenant moves out before the end date without completing paragraph (a), Landlord will not charge the early termination fee. Instead, Tenant remains responsible for rent as it comes due until the earlier of the end date or the date a replacement tenant's rent begins, plus Landlord's reasonable costs of re-renting. Landlord shall take reasonable measures to mitigate the damages recoverable against Tenant, and any rent Landlord receives from a replacement tenant will be credited against what Tenant owes. (735 ILCS 5/9-213.1)
