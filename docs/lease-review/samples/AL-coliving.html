If the total rent that would have come due under this Agreement after the Early Move-Out Date is less than the early termination fee, Tenant pays that smaller amount instead.

When Tenant completes every step in paragraph (a), Tenant owes no rent for any period after the Early Move-Out Date, and the fee is Landlord's only remedy for the early termination itself. The fee does not cover unpaid rent through the Early Move-Out Date, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other charges allowed by this Agreement and Alabama law.

(c) If Tenant leaves early without using this option. Using paragraph (a) is Tenant's choice. If Tenant moves out before the end date without completing paragraph (a), Landlord will not charge the early termination fee. Instead, Tenant remains responsible for rent as it comes due until the earlier of the end date or the date a replacement tenant's rent begins, plus Landlord's reasonable costs of re-renting. If Tenant abandons the Property, Landlord shall make reasonable efforts to re-rent it at a fair rental, although Landlord may first rent its other vacant units.
