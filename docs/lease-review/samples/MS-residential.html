Mississippi Residential Lease Agreement

Governed by Mississippi Residential Landlord and Tenant Act, Miss. Code Ann. §§ 89-8-1 et seq.

1. Parties

Landlord: Sample Landlord LLC (100 Main Street, Suite 2)

Tenant: Jordan Sample (jordan@example.com)

2. Property

Landlord leases to Tenant the residential premises at 123 Sample Ave, Apt 4 (the "Property"). Authorized occupants: Tenant only.

3. Term

This Lease begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027. If Tenant stays after the end date with Landlord's consent (including Landlord's acceptance of rent), the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms. Either party may terminate a month-to-month tenancy by written notice given to the other at least 30 days before the termination date (at least 7 days for a week-to-week tenancy). No termination notice is required when a party has committed a substantial violation of this Lease or the Act that materially affects health or safety. (Miss. Code Ann. § 89-8-19)

4. Rent

Tenant shall pay rent of $1,500.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each month.

Payment methods. Tenant shall pay rent by any of these methods accepted by Landlord: Keycove online payment (ACH or card). Rent is paid on the date Landlord receives the funds.

Late fee. If rent is not paid on time, Landlord may charge a late fee of $75.00. The late fee must be a reasonable estimate of Landlord's costs from the late payment and not a penalty.

5. Security Deposit

Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,500.00. Mississippi law sets no limit on the amount of the security deposit.

Landlord holds the deposit for Tenant, and Tenant's claim to the deposit is prior to the claim of any creditor of Landlord. (Miss. Code Ann. § 89-8-21(2))

By written notice delivered to Tenant, Landlord may claim from the deposit only amounts reasonably necessary to remedy Tenant's defaults in the payment of rent, to repair damage to the premises caused by Tenant (ordinary wear and tear excepted), to clean the premises on termination of the tenancy, or for other reasonable and necessary expenses incurred as a result of Tenant's default; the written notice shall itemize the amounts claimed. Landlord shall return any remaining portion of the deposit no later than 45 days after the termination of the tenancy, delivery of possession, and demand by Tenant. Retaining the deposit or any part in violation of this rule and in the absence of good faith may subject Landlord to damages not exceeding $200 in addition to any actual damages. (Miss. Code Ann. § 89-8-21(3)–(4))

Landlord and Tenant will complete a written move-in condition report, with photographs, and a move-out inspection. Items not noted at move-in are presumed to have been in good condition.

6. Utilities

Utilities included in the rent: Water and trash. Tenant is responsible for arranging and paying for all other utilities.

7. Pets and Parking

Pets allowed: No. Assistance animals for a person with a disability are not pets, and no pet fee or pet deposit applies to them. Parking: One assigned space.

8. Landlord Entry

Mississippi has no statute setting a landlord entry-notice period. Landlord will give Tenant at least 24 hours' notice before entering for inspections, repairs, or showings, except in an emergency or when Tenant consents at the time of entry.

9. Maintenance and Repairs

Landlord shall at all times during the tenancy comply with applicable building and housing codes materially affecting health and safety and maintain the dwelling unit, its plumbing, and its heating and/or cooling system in substantially the same condition as at the start of the Lease (reasonable wear and tear excepted), unless the dwelling unit or a system is damaged or impaired by the deliberate or negligent act of Tenant or of a person on the premises with Tenant's permission. (Miss. Code Ann. § 89-8-23)

Tenant shall keep the Property clean and safe, use appliances and fixtures only for their intended purposes, not damage or allow damage to the Property beyond normal wear and tear, comply with building and housing codes that apply to tenants, and promptly notify Landlord in writing of any needed repair, water leak, or visible mold. Tenant shall not make alterations, paint, or change or add locks without Landlord's written consent, except as the law allows.

10. Use, Assignment, and Subletting

The Property may be used only as a private residence by Tenant and the authorized occupants. Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Property without Landlord's prior written consent. No illegal activity is permitted on the premises.

11. Default and Eviction

If Tenant fails to pay rent, Landlord may deliver a written notice (or, if Tenant has agreed in writing, by email or text message) stating that this Lease will terminate if the rent is not paid within three days. For other material noncompliance by Tenant, Landlord's written notice sets a termination date not less than 14 days after receipt if the breach is not remedied within a reasonable time not exceeding 14 days. (Miss. Code Ann. § 89-8-13(3), (5))

Landlord may recover possession only through the court eviction process. Landlord may commence a residential eviction under §§ 89-8-31 to 89-8-45 for breach of this Lease or the Act (§ 89-8-13) or for failing to vacate after this Lease ends (§§ 89-8-17, 89-8-19), or use the removal-of-tenant procedure under §§ 89-7-1 et seq. Landlord will not remove or exclude Tenant, or shut off utilities, by self-help. (Miss. Code Ann. §§ 89-8-31 to 89-8-45; §§ 89-7-1 et seq.)

12. Early Termination by Tenant (Lease Break)

(a) Tenant's early termination option. Tenant may end this Lease before the end date by doing all of the following: (1) giving Landlord written notice of the date Tenant will move out (the "Early Move-Out Date") at least 30 days before that date; (2) paying all rent and other charges due through the Early Move-Out Date; (3) paying an early termination fee of $3,000.00 on or before the Early Move-Out Date; and (4) moving out and returning all keys and access devices by the Early Move-Out Date. If the total rent that would have come due under this Lease after the Early Move-Out Date is less than the early termination fee, Tenant pays that smaller amount instead.

(b) Liquidated damages, not a penalty. Landlord and Tenant agree that if Tenant leaves early, Landlord's damages (rent lost while the Property is vacant and the cost of advertising, showing, screening, and turnover) are difficult to determine at the time of signing, and that the early termination fee is a reasonable estimate of those damages and not a penalty. When Tenant completes every step in paragraph (a), Tenant owes no rent for any period after the Early Move-Out Date, and the fee is Landlord's only remedy for the early termination itself. The fee does not cover unpaid rent through the Early Move-Out Date, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other charges allowed by this Lease and Mississippi law.

(c) If Tenant leaves early without using this option. Using paragraph (a) is Tenant's choice. If Tenant moves out before the end date without completing paragraph (a), Landlord will not charge the early termination fee. Instead, Tenant remains responsible for rent as it comes due until the earlier of the end date or the date a replacement tenant's rent begins, plus Landlord's reasonable costs of re-renting. If Tenant leaves early, Landlord will make reasonable efforts to re-rent the Property, and rent received from a replacement tenant is credited against what Tenant owes.

(d) Rights protected by law: no early termination fee. Paragraphs (a) through (c) do not apply, and Landlord will not charge the early termination fee, when Tenant ends this Lease under any of the following laws. This Lease cannot waive or reduce these rights, and if any part of this Lease conflicts with them, the law controls. Each right has its own notice and documentation requirements:

  • Military service (federal Servicemembers Civil Relief Act) (50 U.S.C. § 3955). Tenant may terminate this Lease if Tenant enters military service after signing it; or if Tenant, while in military service, signed this Lease and then receives orders for a permanent change of station (which includes separation or retirement) or to deploy with a military unit or in support of a military operation for 90 days or more; or if Tenant signed this Lease upon such orders and then receives a stop movement order of 30 days or more (or indefinite) that prevents occupancy. A spouse or dependent may terminate within one year after Tenant dies in military service or while performing covered National Guard or Reserve duty, and a Tenant who suffers a catastrophic injury or illness in such service (or the spouse or dependent, if Tenant lacks capacity) may terminate within one year after it occurs. Termination also ends the obligations of Tenant's dependents under this Lease. Tenant delivers written notice to Landlord by hand, private business carrier, mail with return receipt requested, or electronic means. Because rent is paid monthly, termination is effective 30 days after the first date on which the next rent payment is due after the notice is delivered; termination after a stop movement order is effective when the notice and orders are delivered. The notice must include a copy of the military orders (official orders, including orders for separation or retirement, or a notification, certification, or verification from the commanding officer). Rent is owed only on a prorated basis through the effective date of termination. Landlord may not impose an early termination charge, but Tenant still owes unpaid amounts due under this Lease, including reasonable charges for excess wear. Rent paid in advance for any period after termination will be refunded within 30 days after the effective date. Landlord may not hold Tenant's belongings or security deposit to satisfy a claim for rent accruing after termination.
  • Death of the tenant (Derrick Beard Act) (Miss. Code Ann. § 89-8-29). If a cosigner signed this Lease and the tenant dies before the Lease ends (or, if there is more than one tenant, all tenants die), the cosigner may terminate this Lease and is presumed to have terminated it. This right applies to leases entered into or renewed after July 1, 2011, and cannot be waived. The cosigner must provide Landlord notice within 30 days of the death — whether the cosigner chooses to terminate or chooses not to terminate the Lease. Termination does not relieve the deceased tenant's estate or the cosigner from paying rent and other sums owed before the death, rent and other sums for the remainder of the month or 30-day period during which the death occurred, and amounts necessary to restore the Property to its condition at the start of the tenancy (ordinary wear and tear excepted). No early termination fee applies.
  • Landlord's material noncompliance (Miss. Code Ann. § 89-8-13(2)–(4)). If Landlord materially fails to comply with this Lease or with Landlord's duties under § 89-8-23 (complying with applicable health-and-safety building and housing codes and maintaining the dwelling unit, its plumbing, and its heating and cooling system), Tenant may terminate this Lease. Tenant delivers Landlord written notice (or, if Landlord has agreed in writing, by email or text message) specifying the acts and omissions and stating that this Lease will terminate on a date not less than 14 days after receipt if the breach is not remedied within a reasonable time not exceeding 14 days. If this Lease terminates, Landlord shall return all prepaid and unearned rent and the security deposit recoverable under § 89-8-21. No early termination fee applies.

(e) Security deposit. After Tenant moves out, Landlord may apply the security deposit to an unpaid early termination fee or to rent owed under paragraph (c) to the extent the law allows, and will account for the deposit as described in the Security Deposit section and as the law requires. Landlord will not apply the deposit to early termination damages where the law prohibits it, including after a termination under a law listed in paragraph (d).
