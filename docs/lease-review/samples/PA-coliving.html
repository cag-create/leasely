Beginning with the second anniversary of the deposit, the deposit must earn interest; Landlord may keep 1% per year as an administrative fee and shall pay Tenant the rest of the interest each year on the anniversary of the lease. (68 P.S. §§ 250.511b, 250.511c (Landlord and Tenant Act §§ 511.2, 511.3)) Landlord shall pay interest on the deposit as required by law.

If the total rent that would have come due under this Agreement after the Early Move-Out Date is less than the early termination fee, Tenant pays that smaller amount instead.

Landlord and Tenant agree that if Tenant leaves early, Landlord's damages (rent lost while the Room is vacant and the cost of advertising, showing, screening, and turnover) are difficult to determine at the time of signing, and that the early termination fee is a reasonable estimate of those damages and not a penalty.
