North Dakota Co-Living / Room Rental Agreement

Room rental in a shared residence. North Dakota landlord-tenant law, including its eviction procedure, applies in full.

1. Parties

Landlord: Sample Landlord LLC (100 Main Street, Suite 2)

Tenant: Jordan Sample (jordan@example.com)

2. Nature of This Agreement

Tenant rents Bedroom B (the "Room") at 123 Sample Ave, Apt 4 (the "Residence") and may use the shared common areas. Authorized occupants of the Room: Tenant only.

Landlord may move Tenant to a comparable room in the Residence only with Tenant's written consent or on at least 30 days' written notice with no increase in rent. The "co-living" and "room rental" terms of this Agreement govern house rules and room assignment only. Tenant has the rights of a residential tenant under North Dakota law. If Landlord seeks to remove Tenant from possession, Landlord will use the court eviction process described in this Agreement and will never use self-help removal.

3. Term

This Agreement begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027. If Tenant stays after the end date with Landlord's consent (including Landlord's acceptance of rent), the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms. Unless the parties have agreed in writing to a longer notice period or different notice time, either Landlord or Tenant may end a month-to-month tenancy by giving the other at least one calendar month's written notice at any time; rent is due up to and including the termination date. If a fixed-term lease has converted to a month-to-month tenancy after expiring, either party may end it on the last day of a month with at least one calendar month's notice. If Landlord changes the terms of a month-to-month tenancy on 30 days' written notice, Tenant may terminate at the end of the month by giving at least 25 days' notice. (N.D. Cent. Code §§ 47-16-07, 47-16-15(2)–(5))

Late fee. If rent is not paid on time, Landlord may charge a late fee of $75.00. The late fee must be a reasonable estimate of Landlord's costs from the late payment and not a penalty.

5. Security Deposit

Landlord may accept up to two months' rent as security from a tenant who has been convicted of a felony (as an incentive to rent to that tenant) or demand up to two months' rent from a tenant who has had a judgment entered against them for violating a previous rental agreement. Separately, Landlord may charge a pet security deposit for an animal that is not a service or companion animal required by a tenant with a disability as a reasonable accommodation; the pet deposit may not exceed the greater of $2,500 or two months' rent. (N.D. Cent. Code § 47-16-07.1(1)–(2))

Landlord will deposit the security deposit in a federally insured, interest-bearing savings or checking account for the benefit of Tenant. If Landlord transfers its ownership interest in the Property, Landlord will transfer the deposit and accrued interest to the new owner, and Landlord is not relieved of liability until it does so. (N.D. Cent. Code § 47-16-07.1(1), (5)) Landlord shall pay interest on the deposit as required by law.

Within 30 days after this Lease terminates and Tenant delivers possession, Landlord will deliver or mail to Tenant, at the last address Tenant furnished to Landlord, the deposit and accrued interest that are not applied, together with a written notice itemizing any amounts applied and stating any amount still due to Landlord or the refund due to Tenant. Interest is not owed if Tenant's occupancy lasted less than nine months. Landlord is liable for treble damages for any deposit withheld without reasonable justification. (N.D. Cent. Code § 47-16-07.1(1), (3)–(4))

When this Lease is entered into, Landlord will provide Tenant a written statement describing the condition of the facilities in and about the Property.
