Iowa Residential Lease Agreement

Governed by Iowa Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Law, Iowa Code ch. 562A

1. Parties

Landlord: Sample Landlord LLC (100 Main Street, Suite 2)

Tenant: Jordan Sample (jordan@example.com)

2. Property

Landlord leases to Tenant the residential premises at 123 Sample Ave, Apt 4 (the "Property"). Authorized occupants: Tenant only.

3. Term

This Lease begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027. If Tenant stays after the end date with Landlord's consent (including Landlord's acceptance of rent), the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms. Either party may terminate a month-to-month tenancy by written notice given to the other at least 30 days before the periodic rental date specified in the notice (at least 10 days for a week-to-week tenancy). (Iowa Code § 562A.34)

4. Rent

Tenant shall pay rent of $1,500.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each month.

Payment methods. Tenant shall pay rent by any of these methods accepted by Landlord: Keycove online payment (ACH or card). Rent is paid on the date Landlord receives the funds.

Late fee. If rent is not paid on time, Landlord may charge a late fee of $75.00. If the monthly rent is $700 or less, a late fee may not exceed $12.00 per day or $60.00 per month in total. If the monthly rent is greater than $700, a late fee may not exceed $20.00 per day or $100.00 per month in total. (Iowa Code § 562A.9(4))

5. Security Deposit

Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,500.00. The security deposit may not exceed two months' rent. (Iowa Code § 562A.12(1))

Landlord shall hold the deposit for Tenant in a bank, savings and loan association, or credit union insured by an agency of the federal government, and shall not commingle it with Landlord's personal funds. The deposit may be held in a trust account, which may be interest-bearing; any interest earned during the first five years of the tenancy is Landlord's property. (Iowa Code § 562A.12(2))

Within 30 days after the tenancy ends and Landlord receives Tenant's mailing address or delivery instructions, Landlord shall return the deposit or furnish Tenant a written statement giving the specific reason for withholding all or part of it (for damage, the nature of the damage). Landlord may withhold only amounts reasonably necessary to remedy unpaid rent or other sums due, to restore the unit to its condition at the start of the tenancy (ordinary wear and tear excepted), or to recover expenses of regaining possession from a tenant who does not act in good faith. A landlord who fails to provide the written statement within that 30 days forfeits the right to withhold any part of the deposit. If Tenant provides no mailing address within one year after the tenancy ends, the deposit becomes Landlord's property. (Iowa Code § 562A.12(3)–(4))

Landlord and Tenant will complete a written move-in condition report, with photographs, and a move-out inspection. Items not noted at move-in are presumed to have been in good condition.

6. Utilities

Utilities included in the rent: Water and trash. Tenant is responsible for arranging and paying for all other utilities.

7. Pets and Parking

Pets allowed: No. Assistance animals for a person with a disability are not pets, and no pet fee or pet deposit applies to them. Parking: One assigned space.

8. Landlord Entry

Landlord may enter the dwelling unit to inspect, make necessary or agreed repairs or improvements, supply necessary or agreed services, or show the unit to prospective or actual purchasers, mortgagees, tenants, workers, or contractors. Except in an emergency or when it is impracticable to do so, Landlord shall give Tenant at least 24 hours' notice and enter only at reasonable times. Tenant shall not unreasonably withhold consent, and Landlord shall not abuse the right of access or use it to harass Tenant. (Iowa Code § 562A.19)

9. Maintenance and Repairs

Landlord shall comply with applicable building and housing codes materially affecting health and safety, make all repairs and do whatever is necessary to put and keep the premises in a fit and habitable condition, keep common areas clean and safe, maintain in good and safe working order all electrical, plumbing, sanitary, heating, ventilating, air-conditioning, and other facilities and appliances Landlord supplies, and supply running water, reasonable hot water, and reasonable heat (except where the unit is so constructed that heat or hot water is generated by an installation within Tenant's exclusive control and supplied by a direct public utility connection). (Iowa Code § 562A.15)

Tenant shall keep the Property clean and safe, use appliances and fixtures only for their intended purposes, not damage or allow damage to the Property beyond normal wear and tear, comply with building and housing codes that apply to tenants, and promptly notify Landlord in writing of any needed repair, water leak, or visible mold. Tenant shall not make alterations, paint, or change or add locks without Landlord's written consent, except as the law allows.

10. Use, Assignment, and Subletting

The Property may be used only as a private residence by Tenant and the authorized occupants. Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Property without Landlord's prior written consent. No illegal activity is permitted on the premises.

11. Default and Eviction

If rent is unpaid when due, Landlord may deliver a written notice stating that the rental agreement will terminate if the rent is not paid within three days; if Tenant does not pay within those three days, Landlord may terminate the rental agreement and bring a forcible entry and detainer action. (Iowa Code § 562A.27(2))

Landlord may recover possession only through the court process (forcible entry and detainer) under Iowa Code chapter 648. Landlord will not remove Tenant, exclude Tenant, or shut off essential services by self-help; an unlawful ouster or willful interruption of services is actionable by Tenant under § 562A.26. (Iowa Code ch. 648; § 562A.26)

12. Early Termination by Tenant (Lease Break)

(a) Tenant's early termination option. Tenant may end this Lease before the end date by doing all of the following: (1) giving Landlord written notice of the date Tenant will move out (the "Early Move-Out Date") at least 30 days before that date; (2) paying all rent and other charges due through the Early Move-Out Date; (3) paying an early termination fee of $3,000.00 on or before the Early Move-Out Date; and (4) moving out and returning all keys and access devices by the Early Move-Out Date. If the total rent that would have come due under this Lease after the Early Move-Out Date is less than the early termination fee, Tenant pays that smaller amount instead.

(b) Liquidated damages, not a penalty. Landlord and Tenant agree that if Tenant leaves early, Landlord's damages (rent lost while the Property is vacant and the cost of advertising, showing, screening, and turnover) are difficult to determine at the time of signing, and that the early termination fee is a reasonable estimate of those damages and not a penalty. When Tenant completes every step in paragraph (a), Tenant owes no rent for any period after the Early Move-Out Date, and the fee is Landlord's only remedy for the early termination itself. The fee does not cover unpaid rent through the Early Move-Out Date, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other charges allowed by this Lease and Iowa law.

(c) If Tenant leaves early without using this option. Using paragraph (a) is Tenant's choice. If Tenant moves out before the end date without completing paragraph (a), Landlord will not charge the early termination fee. Instead, Tenant remains responsible for rent as it comes due until the earlier of the end date or the date a replacement tenant's rent begins, plus Landlord's reasonable costs of re-renting. If Tenant vacates early, Landlord shall make reasonable efforts to re-rent the Property at a fair rental, and rent received from a replacement tenant is credited against what Tenant owes.

(d) Rights protected by law: no early termination fee. Paragraphs (a) through (c) do not apply, and Landlord will not charge the early termination fee, when Tenant ends this Lease under any of the following laws. This Lease cannot waive or reduce these rights, and if any part of this Lease conflicts with them, the law controls. Each right has its own notice and documentation requirements:

  • Military service (federal Servicemembers Civil Relief Act) (50 U.S.C. § 3955). Tenant may terminate this Lease if Tenant enters military service after signing it; or if Tenant, while in military service, signed this Lease and then receives orders for a permanent change of station (which includes separation or retirement) or to deploy with a military unit or in support of a military operation for 90 days or more; or if Tenant signed this Lease upon such orders and then receives a stop movement order of 30 days or more (or indefinite) that prevents occupancy. A spouse or dependent may terminate within one year after Tenant dies in military service or while performing covered National Guard or Reserve duty, and a Tenant who suffers a catastrophic injury or illness in such service (or the spouse or dependent, if Tenant lacks capacity) may terminate within one year after it occurs. Termination also ends the obligations of Tenant's dependents under this Lease. Tenant delivers written notice to Landlord by hand, private business carrier, mail with return receipt requested, or electronic means. Because rent is paid monthly, termination is effective 30 days after the first date on which the next rent payment is due after the notice is delivered; termination after a stop movement order is effective when the notice and orders are delivered. The notice must include a copy of the military orders (official orders, including orders for separation or retirement, or a notification, certification, or verification from the commanding officer). Rent is owed only on a prorated basis through the effective date of termination. Landlord may not impose an early termination charge, but Tenant still owes unpaid amounts due under this Lease, including reasonable charges for excess wear. Rent paid in advance for any period after termination will be refunded within 30 days after the effective date. Landlord may not hold Tenant's belongings or security deposit to satisfy a claim for rent accruing after termination.
  • Military service (Iowa law) (Iowa Code § 29A.101A). A service member who signed this Lease and thereafter enters military service, or who while in military service receives orders for a permanent change of station or to deploy with a military unit (or as an individual in support of a military operation) for 90 days or more, may terminate this Lease for premises occupied or intended to be occupied by the service member or the service member's dependents. Terminating this Lease also ends any obligation a dependent has under it. Tenant delivers to Landlord written notice of termination together with a copy of the service member's military orders, by hand delivery, private business carrier, or U.S. mail with return receipt requested. Because rent is payable monthly, termination is effective 30 days after the first date on which the next rent payment is due after the notice is delivered. The written notice must include a copy of the service member's military orders. Rent for the period before the effective termination date is prorated and paid, and Landlord will not charge any early termination fee. Landlord will refund any rent paid in advance for the period after the effective termination date within 30 days after that date. Knowingly seizing or detaining the service member's or a dependent's property to subject it to a claim for rent accruing after termination is a simple misdemeanor.
  • Fire or casualty damage (Iowa Code § 562A.25). If the dwelling unit or premises are damaged or destroyed by fire or casualty, without Tenant's fault, to an extent that enjoyment of the dwelling unit is substantially impaired, Tenant may terminate this Lease. Tenant may immediately vacate the premises and give Landlord written notice within 14 days of the intention to terminate, in which case this Lease terminates as of the date of vacating. Landlord shall return all prepaid rent and the security deposit recoverable under § 562A.12, with rent accounted for as of the date of the casualty. No early termination fee applies.
  • Unlawful ouster or shutoff of services by Landlord (Iowa Code § 562A.26). If Landlord unlawfully removes or excludes Tenant from the premises, or willfully diminishes services by interrupting or causing the interruption of electric, gas, water, heat, or another essential service, Tenant may terminate this Lease. No advance notice period is required for this right. Tenant may recover possession or terminate this Lease and, in either case, recover actual damages and reasonable attorney's fees as the law allows, and owes no early termination fee.

(e) Security deposit. After Tenant moves out, Landlord may apply the security deposit to an unpaid early termination fee or to rent owed under paragraph (c) to the extent the law allows, and will account for the deposit as described in the Security Deposit section and as the law requires. Landlord will not apply the deposit to early termination damages where the law prohibits it, including after a termination under a law listed in paragraph (d).
