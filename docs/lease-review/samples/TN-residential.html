Tennessee Residential Lease Agreement

Governed by the Tennessee Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act, Tenn. Code Ann. §§ 66-28-101 et seq. (in counties where it applies under § 66-28-102), Tenn. Code Ann. Title 66, Chapter 7 (Leases), and Tenn. Code Ann. Title 29, Chapter 18 (Forcible Entry and Detainer)

1. Parties

Landlord: Sample Landlord LLC (100 Main Street, Suite 2)

Tenant: Jordan Sample (jordan@example.com)

2. Property

Landlord leases to Tenant the residential premises at 123 Sample Ave, Apt 4 (the "Property"). Authorized occupants: Tenant only.

3. Term

This Lease begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027. If Tenant stays after the end date with Landlord's consent (including Landlord's acceptance of rent), the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms. Either party may end a month-to-month tenancy by written notice given to the other at least 30 days before the periodic rent due date specified in the notice as the termination date. A week-to-week tenancy may be ended by written notice given at least 10 days before the termination date specified in the notice. (Tenn. Code Ann. § 66-28-512(a)–(b))

4. Rent

Tenant shall pay rent of $1,500.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each month.

Payment methods. Tenant shall pay rent by any of these methods accepted by Landlord: Keycove online payment (ACH or card). Rent is paid on the date Landlord receives the funds.

Late fee. If rent is not paid on time, Landlord may charge a late fee of $75.00, but only after rent is at least 5 days late. Rent has a five-day grace period, and the day rent is due counts as the first day, so a late fee may first be charged on the sixth day. If the fifth day of the grace period falls on a Sunday or legal holiday, no late fee may be charged if rent is paid on the next business day. A late fee, however described, may not exceed 10% of the amount of rent past due. (Tenn. Code Ann. § 66-28-201(d))

5. Security Deposit

Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,500.00. Tennessee law sets no maximum amount for a security deposit.

Landlord will keep the deposit in an account used only for security deposits, at a bank or other lending institution regulated by the State of Tennessee or an agency of the United States government. When Tenant signs this Lease and pays the deposit, Landlord will tell Tenant in writing where that account is located (Landlord does not have to disclose the account number). (Tenn. Code Ann. § 66-28-301(a), (h))

Tennessee law sets no fixed deadline for refunding the deposit; Landlord agrees to the following. Within 30 days after Tenant vacates and returns possession, Landlord will send to Tenant's last known or reasonably determinable address a written notice of the amount of any refund due, together with an itemized list of any damage charged against the deposit and the estimated cost of repairing it, and will pay any refund due. Landlord may apply the deposit to unpaid rent and other amounts Tenant owes under this Lease. Landlord may charge the deposit for physical damage only if Landlord discovers the damage before the earlier of 30 days after Tenant vacates or seven days after a new tenant takes possession. Tenant has the right to inspect the Property with Landlord to determine any damage charged against the deposit: within five days after Landlord receives Tenant's written notice of intent to vacate, Landlord may notify Tenant of the right to be present at the move-out inspection, which will take place on the day Tenant vacates or within four calendar days afterward, during normal working hours. If Tenant requests a joint inspection, Landlord and Tenant will together list any damage and the estimated cost of repair and sign the list, and Tenant may state in writing any item Tenant disputes. Tenant does not have this inspection right if Tenant vacates without written notice, abandons the Property, is judicially removed, does not respond to Landlord's notice of the right to inspect, fails to appear at the scheduled inspection, or does not request an inspection; in that case Landlord will send Tenant a copy of the damage list and estimated repair costs on Tenant's written request. Landlord will not keep any part of the deposit unless it was held as described above and the damage list was provided as the law requires. (Tenn. Code Ann. § 66-28-301(b)–(g))

Landlord and Tenant will complete a written move-in condition report, with photographs, and a move-out inspection. Items not noted at move-in are presumed to have been in good condition.

Tennessee law sets no general advance-notice period, but Landlord will give Tenant at least 24 hours' notice before any such entry and will enter only at reasonable times, except in an emergency (a sudden, generally unexpected situation demanding immediate action), when Landlord may enter without consent. 12. Early Termination by Tenant (Lease Break)

(a) Tenant's early termination option. Tenant may end this Lease before the end date by doing all of the following: (1) giving Landlord written notice of the date Tenant will move out (the "Early Move-Out Date") at least 30 days before that date; (2) paying all rent and other charges due through the Early Move-Out Date; (3) paying an early termination fee of $3,000.00 on or before the Early Move-Out Date; and (4) moving out and returning all keys and access devices by the Early Move-Out Date. If the total rent that would have come due under this Lease after the Early Move-Out Date is less than the early termination fee, Tenant pays that smaller amount instead.

(b) Liquidated damages, not a penalty. Landlord and Tenant agree that if Tenant leaves early, Landlord's damages (rent lost while the Property is vacant and the cost of advertising, showing, screening, and turnover) are difficult to determine at the time of signing, and that the early termination fee is a reasonable estimate of those damages and not a penalty. When Tenant completes every step in paragraph (a), Tenant owes no rent for any period after the Early Move-Out Date, and the fee is Landlord's only remedy for the early termination itself. The fee does not cover unpaid rent through the Early Move-Out Date, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other charges allowed by this Lease and Tennessee law.

(c) If Tenant leaves early without using this option. Using paragraph (a) is Tenant's choice. If Tenant moves out before the end date without completing paragraph (a), Landlord will not charge the early termination fee. Instead, Tenant remains responsible for rent as it comes due until the earlier of the end date or the date a replacement tenant's rent begins, plus Landlord's reasonable costs of re-renting. If Tenant abandons the Property, Landlord will use reasonable efforts to re-rent it at a fair rental. If Landlord re-rents it for a term beginning before this Lease ends, this Lease terminates on the date the new tenancy begins, and Tenant owes no rent for any period after that date. (Tenn. Code Ann. § 66-28-507(c))

Paragraphs (a) through (c) do not apply, and Landlord will not charge the early termination fee, when Tenant ends this Lease under any of the following laws. This Lease cannot waive or reduce these rights, and if any part of this Lease conflicts with them, the law controls. Each right has its own notice and documentation requirements:

Tenant may terminate this Lease if Tenant enters military service after signing it; or if Tenant, while in military service, signed this Lease and then receives orders for a permanent change of station (which includes separation or retirement) or to deploy with a military unit or in support of a military operation for 90 days or more; or if Tenant signed this Lease upon such orders and then receives a stop movement order of 30 days or more (or indefinite) that prevents occupancy. A spouse or dependent may terminate within one year after Tenant dies in military service or while performing covered National Guard or Reserve duty, and a Tenant who suffers a catastrophic injury or illness in such service (or the spouse or dependent, if Tenant lacks capacity) may terminate within one year after it occurs. Termination also ends the obligations of Tenant's dependents under this Lease. Tenant delivers written notice to Landlord by hand, private business carrier, mail with return receipt requested, or electronic means. Because rent is paid monthly, termination is effective 30 days after the first date on which the next rent payment is due after the notice is delivered; termination after a stop movement order is effective when the notice and orders are delivered. The notice must include a copy of the military orders (official orders, including orders for separation or retirement, or a notification, certification, or verification from the commanding officer). Rent is owed only on a prorated basis through the effective date of termination. Landlord may not impose an early termination charge, but Tenant still owes unpaid amounts due under this Lease, including reasonable charges for excess wear. Rent paid in advance for any period after termination will be refunded within 30 days after the effective date. Landlord may not hold Tenant's belongings or security deposit to satisfy a claim for rent accruing after termination.
  • Domestic abuse, sexual assault, or stalking (Tenn. Code Ann. § 66-7-112; § 66-28-205). Tenant may terminate this Lease if Tenant, or a member of Tenant's family who lives in Tenant's household, is a domestic abuse victim, sexual assault victim, or stalking victim as defined in Tenn. Code Ann. § 36-3-601, whether the victim is an adult or a child. Tenant gives Landlord written notice stating that Tenant or the household member is such a victim and requesting release from this Lease, with a release date agreed by Landlord and Tenant that falls within 30 days after the date of the notice. Tenant must move out within 30 days after giving the notice, or at another time Landlord and Tenant agree. With the notice, Tenant provides either (1) a copy of a valid order of protection issued or extended under Tenn. Code Ann. § 36-3-605 after a hearing at which the court found by a preponderance of the evidence that Tenant or the household member is a domestic abuse, sexual assault, or stalking victim, or (2) documentation of a criminal charge of domestic abuse, sexual assault, or stalking, based on a police report showing that Tenant or the household member was subjected to that conduct. The documentation must be dated no more than 60 days before Tenant's notice. Tenant owes rent for the full month in which the tenancy terminates and any obligations outstanding on the termination date, but no rent for later periods and no early termination fee or penalty. Tenant may terminate this Lease if the Property is damaged or destroyed by fire, the elements, or another casualty so that it is deemed unfit for human habitation by a governmental authority, or so that it is untenantable and unfit for occupancy and the damage occurred without fault or neglect of Tenant. Tenant owes no rent for any period after the earlier of the date Tenant returns the keys or the date Tenant actually moved out, and no early termination fee.   • Unlawful removal or shut-off of essential services by Landlord (URLTA counties) (Tenn. Code Ann. § 66-28-504).   • Unlawful or harassing entry by Landlord (URLTA counties) (Tenn. Code Ann. § 66-28-513(b)).
