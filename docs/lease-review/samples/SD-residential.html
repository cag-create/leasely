Either Landlord or Tenant may end a month-to-month tenancy by giving the other notice at least one month before the end of a monthly rental period. Landlord and Tenant will complete a written move-in condition report, with photographs, and a move-out inspection. Items not noted at move-in are presumed to have been in good condition.

If Tenant fails to pay rent for three days after it is due, Landlord may bring a forcible entry and detainer action. South Dakota law no longer requires a separate written notice to quit before that action is filed. (SDCL 21-16-1(4); SL 2024, ch 75 (repealing SDCL 21-16-2))

Landlord may recover possession of the Property only through a forcible entry and detainer action in circuit court or magistrate court under SDCL chapter 21-16. Landlord will not remove or exclude Tenant from the Property or willfully interrupt electric, gas, water, or other essential services to Tenant. (SDCL 21-16-1, 21-16-3, 21-16-6, 21-16-7; SDCL 43-32-6)

(a) Tenant's early termination option. Tenant may end this Lease before the end date by doing all of the following: (1) giving Landlord written notice of the date Tenant will move out (the "Early Move-Out Date") at least 30 days before that date; (2) paying all rent and other charges due through the Early Move-Out Date; (3) paying an early termination fee of $3,000.00 on or before the Early Move-Out Date; and (4) moving out and returning all keys and access devices by the Early Move-Out Date. If the total rent that would have come due under this Lease after the Early Move-Out Date is less than the early termination fee, Tenant pays that smaller amount instead.

(b) Liquidated damages, not a penalty. Landlord and Tenant agree that if Tenant leaves early, Landlord's damages (rent lost while the Property is vacant and the cost of advertising, showing, screening, and turnover) are difficult to determine at the time of signing, and that the early termination fee is a reasonable estimate of those damages and not a penalty. When Tenant completes every step in paragraph (a), Tenant owes no rent for any period after the Early Move-Out Date, and the fee is Landlord's only remedy for the early termination itself. The fee does not cover unpaid rent through the Early Move-Out Date, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other charges allowed by this Lease and South Dakota law.

(c) If Tenant leaves early without using this option. Using paragraph (a) is Tenant's choice. If Tenant moves out before the end date without completing paragraph (a), Landlord will not charge the early termination fee. Instead, Tenant remains responsible for rent as it comes due until the earlier of the end date or the date a replacement tenant's rent begins, plus Landlord's reasonable costs of re-renting. If Tenant leaves before the end of the term, Landlord will credit against Tenant's liability any rent Landlord receives from a replacement tenant for the remainder of the term.
