South Carolina Co-Living / Room Rental Agreement

Room rental in a shared residence. South Carolina landlord-tenant law, including its eviction procedure, applies in full.

1. Parties

Landlord: Sample Landlord LLC (100 Main Street, Suite 2)

Tenant: Jordan Sample (jordan@example.com)

2. Nature of This Agreement

Tenant rents Bedroom B (the "Room") at 123 Sample Ave, Apt 4 (the "Residence") and may use the shared common areas. Authorized occupants of the Room: Tenant only.

Landlord may move Tenant to a comparable room in the Residence only with Tenant's written consent or on at least 30 days' written notice with no increase in rent. The "co-living" and "room rental" terms of this Agreement govern house rules and room assignment only. Tenant has the rights of a residential tenant under South Carolina law. If Landlord seeks to remove Tenant from possession, Landlord will use the court eviction process described in this Agreement and will never use self-help removal.

3. Term

This Agreement begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027. If Tenant stays after the end date with Landlord's consent (including Landlord's acceptance of rent), the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms. Either Landlord or Tenant may end a month-to-month tenancy by written notice given to the other at least 30 days before the termination date stated in the notice (seven days for a week-to-week tenancy). (S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-770(a)-(b))

4. Rent

Tenant shall pay rent of $1,500.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each month. Rent covers the Room, use of the common areas, and these shared utilities: Water and trash.

Payment methods. Tenant shall pay rent by any of these methods accepted by Landlord: Keycove online payment (ACH or card). Rent is paid on the date Landlord receives the funds.

Late fee. If rent is not paid on time, Landlord may charge a late fee of $75.00. The late fee must be a reasonable estimate of Landlord's costs from the late payment and not a penalty.

5. Security Deposit

Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,500.00. South Carolina law sets no maximum security deposit. If Landlord rents more than four adjoining dwelling units on the premises and uses different standards to calculate deposits for different tenants, Landlord must post the standards in a conspicuous place on the premises or where rent is paid, or give each prospective tenant a written statement of them, before this Lease is signed. (S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-410(c))

When the tenancy ends, Landlord may keep from the security deposit and any prepaid rent only accrued rent and the amount of damages Landlord has suffered because Tenant failed to meet Tenant's maintenance duties under S.C. Code § 27-40-510. Landlord shall send Tenant a written notice itemizing each deduction, together with any amount due, within 30 days after the later of the end of the tenancy and delivery of possession, or Tenant's demand. Tenant shall give Landlord a forwarding address in writing. If Landlord wrongfully withholds any part of the deposit or prepaid rent, Tenant may recover three times the amount wrongfully withheld plus reasonable attorney's fees. (S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-410(a)-(b))

Landlord and Tenant will complete a written move-in condition report, with photographs, and a move-out inspection. Items not noted at move-in are presumed to have been in good condition.

11. Default and Eviction

If rent is not paid within five days after the due date, Landlord may terminate this Lease and begin eviction proceedings. The notice titled "IF YOU DO NOT PAY YOUR RENT ON TIME" in this Lease is Tenant's written notice of nonpayment, and no other notice will be given before an eviction for nonpayment, including during any month-to-month tenancy after the original term. (S.C. Code Ann. §§ 27-40-710(B), 27-37-10(B))

Landlord may recover possession only through an ejectment action in magistrate's court, in which the magistrate issues a rule requiring Tenant to vacate or show cause within 10 days and, if Landlord prevails, a writ of ejectment carried out by a sheriff or constable. Landlord will not remove or exclude Tenant, or interrupt essential services, to regain possession. (S.C. Code Ann. §§ 27-37-10 to 27-37-40, 27-40-760)

12. Early Termination by Tenant (Lease Break)

(a) Tenant's early termination option. Tenant may end this Agreement before the end date by doing all of the following: (1) giving Landlord written notice of the date Tenant will move out (the "Early Move-Out Date") at least 30 days before that date; (2) paying all rent and other charges due through the Early Move-Out Date; (3) paying an early termination fee of $3,000.00 on or before the Early Move-Out Date; and (4) moving out and returning all keys and access devices by the Early Move-Out Date. If the total rent that would have come due under this Agreement after the Early Move-Out Date is less than the early termination fee, Tenant pays that smaller amount instead.

(b) Liquidated damages, not a penalty. Landlord and Tenant agree that if Tenant leaves early, Landlord's damages (rent lost while the Room is vacant and the cost of advertising, showing, screening, and turnover) are difficult to determine at the time of signing, and that the early termination fee is a reasonable estimate of those damages and not a penalty. When Tenant completes every step in paragraph (a), Tenant owes no rent for any period after the Early Move-Out Date, and the fee is Landlord's only remedy for the early termination itself. The fee does not cover unpaid rent through the Early Move-Out Date, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other charges allowed by this Agreement and South Carolina law.

(c) If Tenant leaves early without using this option. Using paragraph (a) is Tenant's choice. If Tenant moves out before the end date without completing paragraph (a), Landlord will not charge the early termination fee. Instead, Tenant remains responsible for rent as it comes due until the earlier of the end date or the date a replacement tenant's rent begins, plus Landlord's reasonable costs of re-renting. If Tenant abandons the Property, Landlord shall make reasonable efforts to re-rent it at a fair rental. If Landlord re-rents it for a term beginning before this Lease would have ended, this Lease ends when the new tenancy begins. If Landlord does not use reasonable efforts to re-rent, or accepts the abandonment as a surrender, this Lease is treated as terminated by Landlord as of the date Landlord had notice of the abandonment. (S.C. Code Ann. § 27-40-730(c))

A spouse or dependent may terminate within one year after Tenant dies in military service or while performing covered National Guard or Reserve duty, and a Tenant who suffers a catastrophic injury or illness in such service (or the spouse or dependent, if Tenant lacks capacity) may terminate within one year after it occurs. Termination also ends the obligations of Tenant's dependents under this Lease. Tenant delivers written notice to Landlord by hand, private business carrier, mail with return receipt requested, or electronic means. Because rent is paid monthly, termination is effective 30 days after the first date on which the next rent payment is due after the notice is delivered; termination after a stop movement order is effective when the notice and orders are delivered. The notice must include a copy of the military orders (official orders, including orders for separation or retirement, or a notification, certification, or verification from the commanding officer). Rent is owed only on a prorated basis through the effective date of termination. Landlord may not hold Tenant's belongings or security deposit to satisfy a claim for rent accruing after termination.

If Landlord does not deliver possession of the Property at the start of the term, rent abates until possession is delivered and Tenant may terminate this Lease.
