2. Nature of This Agreement

Tenant rents Bedroom B (the "Room") at 123 Sample Ave, Apt 4 (the "Residence") and may use the shared common areas. Authorized occupants of the Room: Tenant only.

Landlord may move Tenant to a comparable room in the Residence only with Tenant's written consent or on at least 30 days' written notice with no increase in rent. The "co-living" and "room rental" terms of this Agreement govern house rules and room assignment only. Tenant has the rights of a residential tenant under Maryland law. If Landlord seeks to remove Tenant from possession, Landlord will use the court eviction process described in this Agreement and will never use self-help removal.

3. Term

This Agreement begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027. If Tenant stays after the end date with Landlord's consent (including Landlord's acceptance of rent), the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms. Landlord must give Tenant written notice at least 60 days before the end of a month-to-month tenancy, or at least 60 days before the end of a written lease term longer than one week, to end the tenancy (90 days for a year-to-year tenancy). Tenant may end a month-to-month tenancy by giving Landlord at least 30 days' written notice before the end of a rental period. Any rent increase requires written notice at least 90 days in advance for a tenancy longer than one month, or 60 days in advance for a month-to-month tenancy. (Md. Code, Real Prop. §§ 8-402(c), 8-209)

4. Rent

Tenant shall pay rent of $1,500.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each month. Rent covers the Room, use of the common areas, and these shared utilities: Water and trash.

Payment methods. Tenant shall pay rent by any of these methods accepted by Landlord: Keycove online payment (ACH or card). Rent is paid on the date Landlord receives the funds.

Late fee. If rent is not paid on time, Landlord may charge a late fee of $75.00. A late fee may not exceed 5% of the amount of rent due for the rental period for which payment was late. If rent is paid weekly, the late fee may not exceed $3 per week or a total of $12 per month. (Md. Code, Real Prop. § 8-208(d)(3))

5. Security Deposit

Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,500.00. The security deposit may not exceed one month's rent per dwelling unit, regardless of the number of tenants. The only exception is a deposit of up to two months' rent where Tenant qualifies for utility assistance through the Department of Human Services, this Lease requires Tenant to pay for utility service directly to Landlord, and both parties agree in writing to the amount. Payment of last month's rent in advance counts as part of the security deposit. Landlord may not require more than the security deposit plus the first month's rent for Tenant to begin this Lease and move in. (Md. Code, Real Prop. §§ 8-203(a)–(b), 8-208(d)(11))

Landlord shall give Tenant a written receipt for the security deposit, which is included in this Lease. Within 30 days after receiving it, Landlord shall deposit the security deposit in an interest-bearing account used only for security deposits at a Maryland branch of a federally insured financial institution doing business in Maryland, or hold it in insured certificates of deposit at such a branch or in securities issued by the federal government or the State of Maryland. (Md. Code, Real Prop. §§ 8-203(c)–(d), 8-203.1) Landlord shall pay interest on the deposit as required by law.

Within 45 days after the tenancy ends, Landlord shall return the security deposit by first-class mail to Tenant's last known address, together with simple interest at the greater of 1.5% per year or the daily U.S. Treasury yield curve rate for one year as of the first business day of each year, less any damages rightfully withheld. Interest accrues monthly from the date the deposit was paid, is not compounded, is owed only on deposits of $50 or more held at least six months, and is not owed for any partial month. If any part of the deposit is withheld, Landlord shall send Tenant by first-class mail, within the same 45 days, a written list of the damages claimed with an itemized statement of the costs incurred and supporting documentation (or an estimate, followed by notice and the final invoice when repairs are completed and a refund within 30 days of any amount by which the estimate exceeded actual cost). The deposit may be withheld only for unpaid rent, damage due to breach of this Lease, or damage beyond ordinary wear and tear by Tenant, Tenant's family, agents, employees, guests, or invitees. The deposit is not liquidated damages. (Md. Code, Real Prop. § 8-203(e)–(k))

Tenant has the right to have the Property inspected by Landlord in Tenant's presence, and a written list made of damage existing at the start of the tenancy, if Tenant requests it by certified mail within 15 days after Tenant moves in. Tenant also has the right to be present at the move-out inspection if Tenant notifies Landlord by certified mail at least 15 days before moving of Tenant's intent to move, the moving date, and Tenant's new address; Landlord shall then notify Tenant by certified mail of the inspection date and time, which must be within five days before or after the moving date. If Landlord fails to follow this move-out inspection procedure, Landlord forfeits the right to withhold any part of the deposit for damages.

12. Early Termination by Tenant (Lease Break)

(a) Tenant's early termination option. Tenant may end this Agreement before the end date by doing all of the following: (1) giving Landlord written notice of the date Tenant will move out (the "Early Move-Out Date") at least 30 days before that date; (2) paying all rent and other charges due through the Early Move-Out Date; (3) paying an early termination fee of $3,000.00 on or before the Early Move-Out Date; and (4) moving out and returning all keys and access devices by the Early Move-Out Date. If the total rent that would have come due under this Agreement after the Early Move-Out Date is less than the early termination fee, Tenant pays that smaller amount instead.

(b) Liquidated damages, not a penalty. Landlord and Tenant agree that if Tenant leaves early, Landlord's damages (rent lost while the Room is vacant and the cost of advertising, showing, screening, and turnover) are difficult to determine at the time of signing, and that the early termination fee is a reasonable estimate of those damages and not a penalty. When Tenant completes every step in paragraph (a), Tenant owes no rent for any period after the Early Move-Out Date, and the fee is Landlord's only remedy for the early termination itself. The fee does not cover unpaid rent through the Early Move-Out Date, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other charges allowed by this Agreement and Maryland law.

(c) If Tenant leaves early without using this option. Using paragraph (a) is Tenant's choice. If Tenant moves out before the end date without completing paragraph (a), Landlord will not charge the early termination fee. Instead, Tenant remains responsible for rent as it comes due until the earlier of the end date or the date a replacement tenant's rent begins, plus Landlord's reasonable costs of re-renting. If Tenant moves out before the end of the term, Landlord has a duty to mitigate damages, although Landlord need not show or rent the Property in preference to other available units, and any rent Landlord receives for the Property during the rest of Tenant's term reduces what Tenant owes. This duty cannot be waived. (Md. Code, Real Prop. § 8-207)
