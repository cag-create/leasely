Montana Residential Lease Agreement

Governed by Montana Residential Landlord and Tenant Act of 1977, Mont. Code Ann. §§ 70-24-101 et seq., read together with Residential Tenants' Security Deposits, §§ 70-25-101 et seq.

Landlord: Sample Landlord LLC (100 Main Street, Suite 2)

2. Property

Landlord leases to Tenant the residential premises at 123 Sample Ave, Apt 4 (the "Property"). Authorized occupants: Tenant only.

3. Term

This Lease begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027. If Tenant stays after the end date with Landlord's consent (including Landlord's acceptance of rent), the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms. Either Landlord or Tenant may terminate a month-to-month tenancy at any time by giving the other at least 30 days' written notice before the termination date designated in the notice; the termination date need not fall on a rent due date, and rent is apportioned day to day. A week-to-week tenancy requires at least 7 days' written notice. If this Lease sets no default extension period and neither party gives the other 30 days' written notice to terminate before the original termination date, the tenancy continues month to month. (Mont. Code Ann. §§ 70-24-441, 70-24-205)

4. Rent

Tenant shall pay rent of $1,500.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each month.

Payment methods. Tenant shall pay rent by any of these methods accepted by Landlord: Keycove online payment (ACH or card). Rent is paid on the date Landlord receives the funds.

Late fee. If rent is not paid on time, Landlord may charge a late fee of $75.00. The late fee must be a reasonable estimate of Landlord's costs from the late payment and not a penalty.

5. Security Deposit

Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,500.00. Montana sets no maximum amount for a security deposit. Any fee or charge for cleaning and damages, however labeled, and any deposit Tenant must provide or maintain, is presumed to be a security deposit.

Within 30 days after the tenancy terminates or Landlord accepts Tenant's surrender of the Property, whichever occurs first, Landlord will give Tenant a written list of any rent due and any damage and cleaning charges Landlord claims, together with payment of the balance of the deposit. If, after inspection, there is no damage, no cleaning is required, no rent is unpaid, and Tenant shows that no utilities are unpaid, Landlord will return the full deposit within 10 days. Landlord may deduct only damage caused by Tenant; unpaid rent, late charges, utilities, penalties due under this Lease, and other money owed; and actual cleaning expenses, including a reasonable charge for Landlord's labor. Before deducting cleaning charges, Landlord will give Tenant written notice of the cleaning not done and the cleaning needed, and Tenant will have 24 hours after delivery of that notice to complete it, unless Tenant vacated without notice. At either party's request, the Property may be inspected within one week before the tenancy ends. Landlord will deliver the refund by electronic funds transfer, cash, check, or other form, or by mail to the new address Tenant provides (or Tenant's last-known address). If Landlord fails to provide the written list, Landlord forfeits the right to withhold any part of the deposit for damage or cleaning. (Mont. Code Ann. §§ 70-25-201, 70-25-202, 70-25-203)

Because Landlord requires a security deposit, Landlord will give Tenant, when this Lease is signed, a separate written statement of the present condition of the Property, signed by Landlord or Landlord's agent, describing the condition known to Landlord or that reasonable inspection would reveal, and stating if the Property has never been rented before. At Tenant's written request, Landlord will also give Tenant a copy of the list of damage and cleaning charges provided to the immediately preceding tenant. If Landlord does not provide the statement, Landlord may not recover for damage or cleaning unless it proves by clear and convincing evidence that the damage occurred during this tenancy and was caused by Tenant or Tenant's family, licensees, or invitees.

If the total rent that would have come due under this Lease after the Early Move-Out Date is less than the early termination fee, Tenant pays that smaller amount instead.

(b) Liquidated damages, not a penalty. Landlord and Tenant agree that if Tenant leaves early, Landlord's damages (rent lost while the Property is vacant and the cost of advertising, showing, screening, and turnover) are difficult to determine at the time of signing, and that the early termination fee is a reasonable estimate of those damages and not a penalty. When Tenant completes every step in paragraph (a), Tenant owes no rent for any period after the Early Move-Out Date, and the fee is Landlord's only remedy for the early termination itself. The fee does not cover unpaid rent through the Early Move-Out Date, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other charges allowed by this Lease and Montana law.

(c) If Tenant leaves early without using this option. Using paragraph (a) is Tenant's choice. If Tenant moves out before the end date without completing paragraph (a), Landlord will not charge the early termination fee. Instead, Tenant remains responsible for rent as it comes due until the earlier of the end date or the date a replacement tenant's rent begins, plus Landlord's reasonable costs of re-renting. If Tenant abandons the Property, Landlord will make reasonable efforts to re-rent it at a fair rental. If Landlord re-rents it for a term beginning before this Lease would have expired, this Lease terminates on the date the new tenancy begins, and Tenant owes rent only up to that date. (Mont. Code Ann. §§ 70-24-426(3), 70-24-201(2)(f))
