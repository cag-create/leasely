Room rental in a shared residence. New Jersey landlord-tenant law, including its eviction procedure, applies in full.

Landlord: Sample Landlord LLC (100 Main Street, Suite 2)

2. Nature of This Agreement

Tenant rents Bedroom B (the "Room") at 123 Sample Ave, Apt 4 (the "Residence") and may use the shared common areas. Authorized occupants of the Room: Tenant only.

Landlord may move Tenant to a comparable room in the Residence only with Tenant's written consent or on at least 30 days' written notice with no increase in rent. The "co-living" and "room rental" terms of this Agreement govern house rules and room assignment only. Tenant has the rights of a residential tenant under New Jersey law. If Landlord seeks to remove Tenant from possession, Landlord will use the court eviction process described in this Agreement and will never use self-help removal.

3. Term

This Agreement begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027. If Tenant stays after the end date with Landlord's consent (including Landlord's acceptance of rent), the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms. Tenant may end a month-to-month tenancy by written notice given at least one month before the end of a monthly rental period. Under New Jersey's Anti-Eviction Act, Landlord may not end a month-to-month tenancy, refuse to renew this Lease, or evict Tenant except for one of the good causes listed in N.J.S.A. 2A:18-61.1, after giving the written notice required by N.J.S.A. 2A:18-61.2; the end of the lease term alone is not good cause. If the Property is in an owner-occupied building with no more than two rental units, or is a transient or seasonal rental, the Anti-Eviction Act does not apply and either party may end a month-to-month tenancy by one month's written notice to quit. (N.J.S.A. 2A:18-56, 2A:18-61.1, 2A:18-61.2)

4. Rent

Tenant shall pay rent of $1,500.00 per month, due on the 1st day of each month. Rent covers the Room, use of the common areas, and these shared utilities: Water and trash.

Payment methods. Tenant shall pay rent by any of these methods accepted by Landlord: Keycove online payment (ACH or card). Rent is paid on the date Landlord receives the funds.

Late fee. If rent is not paid on time, Landlord may charge a late fee of $75.00. If Tenant is a senior citizen receiving Social Security old-age, Railroad Retirement, or another governmental pension in lieu of Social Security, or receives Social Security Disability, Supplemental Security Income, or Work First New Jersey benefits, and rent is due on the first of the month, no late fee may be charged unless rent remains unpaid after five business days (excluding Saturdays, Sundays, and state or federal holidays). Any late fee must be reasonable and must not operate as a penalty. (N.J.S.A. 2A:42-6.1, 2A:42-6.3)

5. Security Deposit

Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,500.00. The security deposit may not exceed one and one-half months' rent. Any additional security deposit Landlord collects in a year may not be more than 10% of the current deposit. These deposit rules apply to owner-occupied buildings with no more than two rental units only if Tenant gives Landlord 30 days' written notice invoking them. (N.J.S.A. 46:8-21.2, 46:8-26)

The deposit remains Tenant's property, is held in trust, and may not be commingled with Landlord's property. Landlord shall deposit it in an interest-bearing account at a federally insured bank, savings bank, or savings and loan association in New Jersey (landlords with 10 or more units must use an insured money market fund or a variable-rate account as the statute specifies). Within 30 days after receiving the deposit, Landlord shall notify Tenant in writing of the name and address of the institution, the type of account, the current interest rate, and the amount of the deposit, and shall give the same notice within 30 days after moving the deposit, at each annual interest payment, and within 30 days after any transfer of ownership. Interest belongs to Tenant and is paid in cash or credited toward rent each year on the lease anniversary or renewal (or January 31 if Landlord has given notice). If Landlord fails to deposit the money, give notice, or pay interest as required, Tenant may give written notice to apply the deposit plus 7% annual interest toward rent. (N.J.S.A. 46:8-19) Landlord shall pay interest on the deposit as required by law.

Within 30 days after this Lease ends, Landlord shall return the deposit plus Tenant's share of the interest or earnings, less any charges permitted by this Lease, by personal delivery or registered or certified mail, with an itemized statement of the interest and each deduction sent the same way. No deduction may be made while Tenant remains in possession. If Tenant is displaced by fire, flood, condemnation, or evacuation and the premises are posted or certified as uninhabitable, Landlord shall make the deposit available within five business days; after a termination under the Safe Housing Act, within 15 business days. If a court finds Landlord wrongfully withheld the deposit, Tenant recovers double the amount withheld plus costs and, in the court's discretion, attorney's fees. (N.J.S.A. 46:8-21.1)

Landlord and Tenant will complete a written move-in condition report, with photographs, and a move-out inspection. Items not noted at move-in are presumed to have been in good condition.

New Jersey law does not require a notice to quit before a nonpayment case, but Landlord may not remove Tenant without a court judgment for possession and a warrant of removal. (N.J.S.A. 2A:18-61.1(a), 2A:18-61.2)

Landlord may remove Tenant only through a summary dispossess action in the Superior Court, Law Division, Special Civil Part, Landlord/Tenant Section, and (unless an exemption applies) only for a good cause listed in the Anti-Eviction Act, followed by a judgment for possession and a warrant of removal executed by a court officer. Landlord will not enter or retake the Property without Tenant's consent except through that legal process; a lockout is a disorderly persons offense. (N.J.S.A. 2A:18-53 et seq., 2A:18-61.1, 2A:18-61.2, 2A:39-1)
