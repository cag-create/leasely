12. Early Termination by Tenant (Lease Break)

(a) Tenant's early termination option. Tenant may end this Lease before the end date by doing all of the following: (1) giving Landlord written notice of the date Tenant will move out (the "Early Move-Out Date") at least 30 days before that date; (2) paying all rent and other charges due through the Early Move-Out Date; (3) paying an early termination fee of $3,000.00 on or before the Early Move-Out Date; and (4) moving out and returning all keys and access devices by the Early Move-Out Date. If the total rent that would have come due under this Lease after the Early Move-Out Date is less than the early termination fee, Tenant pays that smaller amount instead.

(b) Liquidated damages, not a penalty. Landlord and Tenant agree that if Tenant leaves early, Landlord's damages (rent lost while the Property is vacant and the cost of advertising, showing, screening, and turnover) are difficult to determine at the time of signing, and that the early termination fee is a reasonable estimate of those damages and not a penalty. When Tenant completes every step in paragraph (a), Tenant owes no rent for any period after the Early Move-Out Date, and the fee is Landlord's only remedy for the early termination itself. The fee does not cover unpaid rent through the Early Move-Out Date, damage beyond normal wear and tear, or other charges allowed by this Lease and Oklahoma law.

(c) If Tenant leaves early without using this option. Using paragraph (a) is Tenant's choice. If Tenant moves out before the end date without completing paragraph (a), Landlord will not charge the early termination fee. Instead, Tenant remains responsible for rent as it comes due until the earlier of the end date or the date a replacement tenant's rent begins, plus Landlord's reasonable costs of re-renting. If Tenant wrongfully abandons the Property during the term, Landlord will make reasonable efforts to rent it. If Landlord rents the Property for a term beginning before this Lease ends, this Lease terminates when the new tenancy begins. If, after reasonable efforts, Landlord cannot re-rent the Property for a fair rental, Tenant is liable for the rent, or the difference in rent, for the remainder of the term. (41 O.S. §§ 105(A), 129(B))

A spouse or dependent may terminate within one year after Tenant dies in military service or while performing covered National Guard or Reserve duty, and a Tenant who suffers a catastrophic injury or illness in such service (or the spouse or dependent, if Tenant lacks capacity) may terminate within one year after it occurs. Termination also ends the obligations of Tenant's dependents under this Lease. Tenant delivers written notice to Landlord by hand, private business carrier, mail with return receipt requested, or electronic means. Because rent is paid monthly, termination is effective 30 days after the first date on which the next rent payment is due after the notice is delivered; termination after a stop movement order is effective when the notice and orders are delivered. The notice must include a copy of the military orders (official orders, including orders for separation or retirement, or a notification, certification, or verification from the commanding officer). Rent is owed only on a prorated basis through the effective date of termination. Landlord may not impose an early termination charge, but Tenant still owes unpaid amounts due under this Lease, including reasonable charges for excess wear. Rent paid in advance for any period after termination will be refunded within 30 days after the effective date. Landlord may not hold Tenant's belongings or security deposit to satisfy a claim for rent accruing after termination.
  • Oklahoma military forces on state active duty (44 O.S. § 208.1; 50 U.S.C. § 3955). A member of the Oklahoma state military forces who is ordered to state active duty, or to Title 32 active duty under 32 U.S.C. §§ 501 through 507, has the same lease termination protections that the federal Servicemembers Civil Relief Act gives to servicemembers in federal military service. Tenant gives Landlord written notice of termination.   • Uninhabitable conditions or imminent threat to health and safety (41 O.S. § 121(D)). Tenant gives Landlord notice of Tenant's intention to terminate. Landlord will return all recoverable deposits and all prepaid and unearned rent, and Tenant may recover up to twice the average monthly rent or twice Tenant's actual damages, whichever is greater.
  • Unlawful entry or harassment by Landlord (41 O.S. § 124). If Landlord makes an unlawful entry, makes a lawful entry in an unreasonable manner, or harasses Tenant by making repeated unreasonable demands for entry, Tenant may terminate this Lease.
