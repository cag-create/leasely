2. Nature of This Agreement

Tenant rents Bedroom B (the "Room") at 123 Sample Ave, Apt 4 (the "Residence") and may use the shared common areas. Authorized occupants of the Room: Tenant only.

Landlord may move Tenant to a comparable room in the Residence only with Tenant's written consent or on at least 30 days' written notice with no increase in rent. The "co-living" and "room rental" terms of this Agreement govern house rules and room assignment only. Tenant has the rights of a residential tenant under Wisconsin law. If Landlord seeks to remove Tenant from possession, Landlord will use the court eviction process described in this Agreement and will never use self-help removal.

3. Term

This Agreement begins on January 1, 2027 and ends on December 31, 2027. If Tenant stays after the end date with Landlord's consent (including Landlord's acceptance of rent), the tenancy continues month-to-month on the same terms. Either party may end a month-to-month tenancy by written notice given at least 28 days before the end of a rental period; the tenancy can end only at the end of a rental period. If rent is payable more often than monthly, notice at least equal to the rent-paying period is sufficient. (Wis. Stat. § 704.19)

Payment methods. Tenant shall pay rent by any of these methods accepted by Landlord: Keycove online payment (ACH or card). Rent is paid on the date Landlord receives the funds.

5. Security Deposit

Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,500.00. Wisconsin law sets no maximum security deposit, but any rent paid in advance beyond one month's rent is treated as part of the security deposit. (Wis. Admin. Code ATCP 134.02(11), 134.06(2) note)

Immediately upon accepting any earnest money or security deposit, Landlord shall give Tenant a written receipt stating the nature and amount of the deposit (not required for a check that notes its purpose, unless Tenant requests one). Landlord shall give Tenant a written receipt for any rent paid in cash. (Wis. Admin. Code ATCP 134.03(2))

Landlord shall deliver or mail to Tenant the full deposit, less any amounts lawfully withheld, within 21 days after: the Lease termination date, if Tenant vacates on that date; the termination date or, if Landlord re-rents sooner, the date the new tenancy begins, if Tenant vacates or is evicted early; or the date Landlord learns Tenant has vacated or been removed, if Tenant leaves after the termination date. If any amount is withheld, Landlord shall deliver or mail within the same period a written statement describing each item of damage or other claim and the amount withheld for each. Landlord may withhold only amounts reasonably necessary to pay for Tenant damage, waste, or neglect; unpaid rent (subject to Landlord's duty to mitigate); unpaid charges for utility service provided by Landlord but not included in rent; unpaid government-owned utility charges and unpaid municipal permit fees for which Landlord becomes liable; and any other reason stated in a separately signed NONSTANDARD RENTAL PROVISIONS document. Landlord may not withhold for normal wear and tear. (Wis. Stat. § 704.28; Wis. Admin. Code ATCP 134.06(2)–(4))

When Tenant's occupancy begins, Landlord shall give Tenant a check-in sheet, and Tenant has 7 days from the start of occupancy to note the condition of the Premises and return it to Landlord. Before accepting the security deposit, Landlord shall notify Tenant in writing that, by a deadline at least 7 days after the tenancy begins, Tenant may inspect the Premises and report any pre-existing damage or defects, and may request a list of physical damages or defects charged to the previous tenant's deposit. Landlord shall provide that list within 30 days after the request, or within 7 days after notifying the previous tenant of deductions, whichever is later.
