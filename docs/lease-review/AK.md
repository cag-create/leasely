## Early termination rights that override the lease-break fee

### Military service (federal Servicemembers Civil Relief Act)
*50 U.S.C. § 3955*

- **Who:** Tenant may terminate this Lease if Tenant enters military service after signing it; or if Tenant, while in military service, signed this Lease and then receives orders for a permanent change of station (which includes separation or retirement) or to deploy with a military unit or in support of a military operation for 90 days or more; or if Tenant signed this Lease upon such orders and then receives a stop movement order of 30 days or more (or indefinite) that prevents occupancy. A spouse or dependent may terminate within one year after Tenant dies in military service or while performing covered National Guard or Reserve duty, and a Tenant who suffers a catastrophic injury or illness in such service (or the spouse or dependent, if Tenant lacks capacity) may terminate within one year after it occurs. Termination also ends the obligations of Tenant's dependents under this Lease.
- **Notice:** Tenant delivers written notice to Landlord by hand, private business carrier, mail with return receipt requested, or electronic means. Because rent is paid monthly, termination is effective 30 days after the first date on which the next rent payment is due after the notice is delivered; termination after a stop movement order is effective when the notice and orders are delivered.
- **Documentation:** The notice must include a copy of the military orders (official orders, including orders for separation or retirement, or a notification, certification, or verification from the commanding officer).
- **Owes:** Rent is owed only on a prorated basis through the effective date of termination. Landlord may not impose an early termination charge, but Tenant still owes unpaid amounts due under this Lease, including reasonable charges for excess wear. Rent paid in advance for any period after termination will be refunded within 30 days after the effective date. Landlord may not hold Tenant's belongings or security deposit to satisfy a claim for rent accruing after termination.

### Alaska organized militia on state active duty
*AS 26.05.135(a); AS 26.05.010(b); 50 U.S.C. § 3955*

- **Who:** Alaska law applies the consumer-transaction and lease protections of the federal Servicemembers Civil Relief Act to members of the organized militia (the Alaska National Guard, the Alaska Naval Militia, and the Alaska State Defense Force) while on active duty for the state by order of the governor. Such a Tenant may terminate this Lease on the same terms the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act provides for federal military service, including after entering that duty during the term of this Lease or, if Tenant signed this Lease while on that duty, after receiving orders for a permanent change of station or a deployment of at least 90 days.
- **Notice:** Tenant delivers written notice of termination to Landlord or Landlord's agent by hand delivery, private business carrier, return-receipt mail, or an agreed electronic method. For a lease with monthly rent, termination takes effect 30 days after the first date on which the next rental payment is due after the notice is delivered.
- **Documentation:** The notice must include a copy of the governor's order or other military orders placing Tenant on state active duty.
- **Owes:** Unpaid rent for the period before the termination date is prorated. Landlord may not impose an early termination charge, but Tenant remains responsible for other obligations due under this Lease at termination, including reasonable charges for excess wear. Rent paid in advance for any period after termination will be refunded within 30 days.

### Landlord's material noncompliance
*AS 34.03.160(a), (d)*

- **Who:** If Landlord materially fails to comply with this Lease, or fails to comply with AS 34.03.100 in a way that materially affects health and safety, Tenant may terminate this Lease. Tenant may not terminate for a condition caused by the deliberate or negligent act or omission of Tenant, Tenant's family, or others on the premises with Tenant's consent.
- **Notice:** Tenant gives Landlord written notice specifying the breach and stating that this Lease will terminate on a date at least 20 days after Landlord receives the notice if the breach is not remedied within 10 days. If Landlord remedies the breach before the termination date, this Lease does not terminate. If substantially the same breach recurs within six months without due care by Landlord, Tenant may terminate on at least 10 days' written notice specifying the breach and the termination date.
- **Owes:** Tenant owes rent only through the termination date, and Landlord will return all prepaid rent and security deposit recoverable under AS 34.03.070.

### Failure to deliver possession
*AS 34.03.170*

- **Who:** If Landlord fails to deliver possession of the dwelling unit at the start of the term, rent abates until possession is delivered and Tenant may terminate this Lease.
- **Notice:** Tenant gives Landlord at least 10 days' written notice of termination.
- **Owes:** Tenant owes no rent, and Landlord will return all prepaid rent and security deposits.

### Fire or casualty damage
*AS 34.03.200*

- **Who:** If the dwelling unit or premises are damaged or destroyed by fire or casualty so that Tenant's enjoyment of the unit is substantially impaired, Tenant may terminate this Lease.
- **Notice:** Tenant immediately vacates the premises and notifies Landlord of the intention to terminate; this Lease terminates as of the date Tenant vacates.
- **Owes:** Rent is accounted for as of the date of the casualty, and Landlord will return all prepaid rent and security deposits recoverable under AS 34.03.070.

### Unlawful ouster or interruption of essential services
*AS 34.03.210*

- **Who:** If Landlord unlawfully removes or excludes Tenant from the Property, or wilfully diminishes services by interrupting or causing the interruption of electric, gas, water, sanitary, or other essential service, Tenant may recover possession or terminate this Lease.
- **Notice:** Tenant may terminate by notice to Landlord.
- **Owes:** Tenant may recover up to one and one-half times actual damages, and Landlord will return all prepaid rent and security deposits recoverable under AS 34.03.070.

### Landlord's abuse of access
*AS 34.03.300(b)*

- **Who:** If Landlord makes an unlawful entry, makes a lawful entry in an unreasonable manner, or makes repeated demands for entry that unreasonably harass Tenant, Tenant may terminate this Lease.
- **Notice:** Tenant gives Landlord written notice at least 10 days before the termination date specified in the notice.
- **Owes:** Tenant may recover the greater of actual damages or one month's rent, plus court costs and reasonable attorney fees.

### Improper refusal of a sublease or assignment
*AS 34.03.060(c)-(e)*

- **Who:** If Tenant presents a prospective subtenant or assignee with a signed written offer containing the information listed in AS 34.03.060(c), and Landlord's written rejection does not state one of the grounds permitted by AS 34.03.060(d), Tenant may treat Landlord's consent as given or may terminate this Lease.
- **Notice:** Tenant gives Landlord written notice without unnecessary delay, at least 30 days before the termination date specified in the notice.
- **Owes:** Tenant owes rent through the termination date.


## State disclosures in the lease

- **Owner and Manager Disclosure** (AS 34.03.080(a), (b)): The person authorized to manage the Property, and the owner or person authorized to act for the owner for service of process and for receiving and receipting for notices and demands, is {{landlord_name}}, {{landlord_address}}. Landlord will keep this information current.
- **Notice of Extended Absence** (AS 34.03.150; AS 34.03.230(a), (b)): Tenant will notify Landlord of any anticipated absence from the Property of more than seven days, as soon as reasonably possible after Tenant knows the absence will exceed seven days. If Tenant wilfully fails to do so, Landlord may recover up to one and one-half times Landlord's actual damages. During an absence of more than seven days, Landlord may enter the unit at times reasonably necessary.
- **Terms for Withholding Prepaid Rent and Deposit** (AS 34.03.070(b), (c), (h)): At the end of the tenancy, Landlord may apply prepaid rent and the security deposit only to accrued rent and to damages to the premises and any contents caused by Tenant's failure to meet Tenant's obligations under AS 34.03.120, excluding normal wear and tear. Any pet deposit may be applied only to damages directly related to Tenant's pet.
- **Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Detectors** (AS 18.70.095; AS 34.03.100(a)(7); AS 34.03.120(a)(7)): At the start of the tenancy, Landlord will provide working smoke detection devices and, if the unit contains or is served by a carbon-based-fueled appliance, has an attached garage or carport, or is adjacent to a parking space, working carbon monoxide detection devices, and will repair or replace them after Tenant notifies Landlord of a deficiency. Tenant will keep the devices in working condition by keeping charged batteries in battery-operated devices, testing them periodically, and not permanently disabling them.
- **No Landlord-Supplied Running Water** (AS 34.03.100(a)(5)(B)) *Only if the Property has no well and no water provided by a direct public utility connection, and the parties agree to waive Landlord's duty to supply running water or hot water*: The Property does not have a well or water provided by a direct public utility connection, and Tenant waives Landlord's duty to supply running water or hot water to the Property.

## Open questions for the reviewing attorney

- No domestic violence early-termination statute: the full text of AS 34.03 was read and contains no DV, sexual assault, or stalking termination right, and none was found elsewhere. Several commercial guides cite nonexistent AS 34.03 sections for this; disregard them. Federal VAWA applies only to covered federally assisted housing. domesticViolence is null.
- Military: AS 26.05.135(a) makes the SCRA's 'consumer transactions and temporary suspension of enforcement of civil liabilities' provisions apply to Alaska organized militia (National Guard, Naval Militia, State Defense Force) on active duty for the state by order of the governor. I read this as extending 50 U.S.C. § 3955 lease termination to state active duty, which the federal SCRA alone does not cover, but the statute incorporates the SCRA by reference and does not restate lease terms; attorney should confirm. AS 26.05.135(b) (internet, gym, satellite radio, TV contracts) does not cover leases.
- Deposit cap of two months does not apply at all when monthly rent exceeds $2,000 (AS 34.03.070(a)); the builder should not show a cap for those units. Separate pet deposit of up to one month is allowed (AS 34.03.070(h)) but not for service animals. The return deadline is 14 days only if proper AS 34.03.290 notice was given and no damage deductions are taken; otherwise 30 days.
- No statutory late-fee cap or grace period in AS 34.03. A late fee is governed by general contract law and must not be an unenforceable penalty; AS 34.03.040(a)(4) also voids any lease clause making the tenant pay the landlord's attorney fees, although AS 34.03.350 lets a court award fees to the prevailing party.
- Required lease clause: AS 34.03.150 says the rental agreement SHALL require the tenant to notify the landlord of an anticipated absence over seven days; included as a disclosure. AS 34.03.040(a) also prohibits waiver, confession of judgment, exculpation/indemnity, and attorney-fee clauses.
- Fair housing: AS 18.80.240 lists sex, marital status, changes in marital status, pregnancy, race, religion, disability, color, and national origin for rental refusals and terms, and adds age only for listing and inspection practices; it contains no explicit familial status protection (federal law applies) and expressly permits 'housing for singles or married couples only,' which conflicts with the federal Fair Housing Act's familial status rule; do not rely on that exception. Anchorage has a local equal-rights ordinance that adds sexual orientation and gender identity (not read here; verify), and other municipalities may as well; the Alaska State Commission for Human Rights' interpretation of 'sex' to include those classes has changed over time.
- No statewide rent control, just cause, or required environmental disclosures (radon, mold, flood) were found in AS 34.03.
